Asana Task Title:
Tighten Product Page Purchase Area
Task Description:
Update the desktop product page layout to make the purchase area on the right side more compact, focused, and easier to scan. The goal is to reduce unnecessary vertical spacing between the key buying elements so customers can understand the product, delivery options, and add-to-cart action faster.
Current Issue:
The product purchase section feels slightly too spread out. The price, title, delivery options, add-to-cart button, and trust cards have generous spacing, which makes the buying area feel longer than necessary. This pushes some reassurance/payment information lower and makes the decision flow feel less direct.
Required Changes:
Reduce vertical spacing in the right-side purchase column
Tighten the gap between:
Price
Tax inclusive label
Earn points label
Product title
Delivery options label
Delivery option cards
Add to Cart button
Trust/reassurance cards
Keep the layout elegant and premium, but make the section feel more compact.
Bring price, tax label, and points closer together
Keep $65, Tax Inclusive, and Earn 26 Points visually connected.
Avoid placing the points label too far away from the price.
The price area should feel like one clear product value section.
Move the product title closer to the price
Reduce the gap between the price row and product title.
The product title should feel directly connected to the price, not like a separate section.
Tighten the delivery options section
Reduce the spacing above and below the Delivery Options label.
Slightly reduce the height/padding of the delivery option cards.
Keep the selected state for Express Delivery, but make the card less tall.
Keep the unselected scheduled delivery card consistent and compact.
Keep the Add to Cart button prominent
The button should remain visually strong and easy to find.
Reduce excess spacing above and below the button.
Do not make the button feel smaller or weaker; only tighten the surrounding layout.
Compact the trust cards below the button
Reduce vertical padding inside the trust cards:
Free Standard Delivery
No Address Hassle
Live Order Tracking
Reduce the space between these cards.
Keep icons, titles, and subtitles readable.
Maintain the premium look
Do not make the section feel cramped.
Keep rounded corners, soft borders, clean typography, and the current luxury visual style.
The final result should feel cleaner, faster, and more conversion-focused.
Acceptance Criteria:
The right-side purchase area appears noticeably tighter than the current version.
The price, title, delivery options, and add-to-cart button are visible as one cohesive purchase block.
No content is removed.
The Add to Cart button remains dominant.
The delivery option cards and trust cards remain readable.
The page still feels premium and spacious, but with less wasted vertical space.
Reference Direction:
Use the generated mockup as the visual direction: the same product page structure, but with a more compact purchase column where all buying elements are closer together and easier to scan.
