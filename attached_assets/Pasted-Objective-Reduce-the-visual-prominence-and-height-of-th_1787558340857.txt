Objective
Reduce the visual prominence and height of the optional sign-in section on the mobile Delivery Details checkout step, while keeping all existing authentication methods available.

Scope
Mobile checkout only. Update only the sign-in card located directly below the “Delivery Details” heading. Do not change Recipient Details, Delivery, Sender Details, Order Summary, or the sticky payment CTA as part of this task.

Approved UI
• Heading: “Already have an account?”
• Supporting text: “Sign in to use saved addresses and track orders.”
• Show two equal-width outlined pills in one row:
  – Apple icon + “Apple”
  – Google icon + “Google”
• Keep “Sign in with email” as a centered secondary text link below the pills.
• Remove “Continue as guest ↓”. The Recipient Details form immediately below remains the guest-checkout path.
• Reduce vertical gaps and lower padding so the card is approximately 20–25% shorter than the previous compact version and substantially shorter than the current production card.
• Preserve Presentail’s existing serif heading style, colors, border radius, border treatment, shadows, and page gutters.

Layout requirements
• Use approximately 16px internal card padding.
• Apple and Google pills must be equal width, aligned, and at least 44px high.
• Maintain a clear but compact gap between the supporting copy, pills, and email link.
• The card must use the same left and right alignment as the surrounding checkout cards.
• The Recipient Details card should begin immediately after the standard inter-card gap.
• Test at 320px, 375px, 390px, and 430px viewport widths. Do not allow text, icons, or buttons to wrap or overflow.

Functional requirements
• Apple pill must trigger the existing Sign in with Apple flow.
• Google pill must trigger the existing Google authentication flow.
• “Sign in with email” must trigger the existing email sign-in flow.
• Preserve the customer’s cart, selected delivery slot, recipient data already entered, currency, country, language, and checkout position after successful authentication or cancellation.
• Authentication errors and cancellation must return the customer safely to the same Delivery Details step without clearing checkout data.
• Guest checkout must remain fully available simply by continuing with the form below.

Accessibility
• Use semantic buttons/links with accessible names.
• Maintain a minimum 44×44px touch target.
• Preserve visible keyboard focus states.
• Icons must not be the sole accessible label.
• Confirm text and borders meet existing checkout contrast standards.

Analytics
Preserve existing authentication analytics. Confirm events continue to distinguish Apple, Google, email, success, cancellation, and failure. Do not introduce duplicate events.

Acceptance criteria
1. The sign-in card matches the attached approved mockup.
2. The exact approved copy is used.
3. Apple and Google appear as equal-width compact pills in one row.
4. Email remains available as a secondary text link.
5. “Continue as guest ↓” is removed.
6. The card is materially shorter and the Recipient Details form appears higher on the page.
7. All three sign-in methods work and preserve checkout state.
8. Guest checkout is unaffected.
9. No other checkout sections are visually or functionally changed.
10. QA passes on current iOS Safari and Android Chrome across the listed widths.
