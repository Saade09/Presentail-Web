OBJECTIVE

Improve the checkout payment page hierarchy so customers can see the payment action immediately, review delivery information before completing payment, and understand optional order instructions without cluttering the checkout.

APPROVED DESIGN SCOPE

1. Bring the payment CTA above the fold
- Remove the large, open order-instructions textarea from above the payment methods.
- Slightly reduce vertical spacing within and between payment-method rows.
- Keep the existing white Google Pay button exactly as it is today; do not change its styling or add the total to the button.
- The full selected payment CTA must be visible without scrolling at the target desktop viewport shown in the reference mockup.

2. Update the introductory message
- Keep the heading: “Payment”.
- Replace the existing redirect-specific description with:
  “Choose a secure payment method to complete your order.”
- This wording must remain accurate for Google Pay, card, PayPal, and Whish Money.

3. Add payment reassurance
- Directly beneath the payment CTA, show a subtle lock icon and:
  “Secure encrypted payment”
- This reassurance must be visually secondary to the payment CTA.

4. Move and improve order instructions
- Position the order-instructions component below the payment CTA and security reassurance.
- Default state must be collapsed.
- Collapsed label:
  “Order instructions (optional)”
- Show a plus icon when collapsed.
- When expanded, show the helper text:
  “Share any special preparation or packaging requests with our team.”
- Textarea placeholder:
  “E.g., Please arrange all items together as one gift, if possible.”
- Do not use examples about removing price tags.
- Gift-card messages and delivery instructions must remain in their existing dedicated fields and must not be duplicated here.

5. Add a final delivery recap
- Place a compact Delivery details card directly below the Order instructions component in the left checkout column.
- Do not place this information inside the Order Summary.
- Show:
  • Recipient
  • Shortened delivery address or address-collection status
  • Delivery method
  • Delivery date and selected time window
- Example presentation:
  Delivery details                                      Edit
  To: Maya Haddad
  Hamra, Beirut, Lebanon
  Express delivery · Today, 10:30–11:00 PM
- Include restrained recipient, location, and time icons.
- The Edit link must return the customer to the Delivery Details step and preserve all previously entered checkout information.

6. Support dynamic delivery-recap states
- Saved address: show the shortened saved address.
- Recipient will provide address: show “We’ll contact [recipient first name] to confirm the delivery address.”
- Customer is the recipient: use “Delivering to you.”
- Standard delivery: show “Standard delivery” and the selected delivery window.
- Express delivery: show “Express delivery” and the applicable arrival window.
- Scheduled delivery: show the exact selected date and time slot.
- Do not repeat the delivery fee inside this recap.

7. Remove redundant navigation
- Remove the large bottom “Back to delivery details” button.
- The contextual Edit link in the Delivery details card becomes the control for revising delivery information.
- Keep “Back to Cart” in the header unchanged.

8. Keep the Order Summary focused
- The right column should continue to contain products, quantities, prices, discounts, coupon/gift-card entry, subtotal, delivery charges, and total.
- Do not add recipient, address, or delivery-time details to the Order Summary.
- Do not change pricing, discount, delivery-fee, or total calculations as part of this task.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- On desktop, the full selected payment CTA is visible without scrolling at the reference viewport.
- The existing Google Pay button design and wording are unchanged.
- Payment methods remain selectable and all existing payment flows continue to work.
- The new introductory message is displayed exactly as approved.
- “Secure encrypted payment” appears beneath the CTA.
- Order instructions are collapsed by default and expand/collapse correctly.
- The approved order-instructions label, helper copy, and placeholder are used exactly.
- The Delivery details recap appears below Order instructions in the left column.
- The recap renders the correct state for saved address, recipient-address collection, self-delivery, standard, express, and scheduled orders.
- Edit returns to Delivery Details without clearing customer, recipient, address, delivery, note, cart, or payment-selection data.
- The redundant bottom Back to delivery details button is removed.
- The right Order Summary remains dedicated to products and financial information.
- Existing responsive behavior is preserved and there are no layout regressions on mobile or desktop.
- Long recipient names, addresses, translated text, and delivery windows wrap cleanly without overlapping controls.
- No changes are made to payment processing, price calculations, delivery calculations, or checkout data persistence beyond what is required above.

QA CHECKS

- Test Google Pay, Credit/Debit Card, PayPal, and Whish Money selections.
- Test order instructions collapsed, expanded, empty, and populated.
- Test saved address, recipient-provided address, self-delivery, standard delivery, express delivery, and scheduled delivery.
- Test a long recipient name and long address.
- Test a cart with one item and a cart with several items.
- Verify browser refresh/back navigation does not unexpectedly clear entered checkout data.
- Verify the payment action cannot be obscured by the instructions or delivery recap.
