We want to improve how categories and occasions are ordered on the Presentail homepage. Instead of sorting them alphabetically or manually only, we should introduce a smarter sorting system that prioritizes what is most likely to convert.

The goal is to automatically rank homepage rows such as Shop by Category and Shop by Occasion based on customer behavior, business value, seasonality, and product availability.

The sorting should help ensure that the first visible items in each row are the most relevant and commercially valuable, especially on mobile where users only see a few items before scrolling.


Recommended Sorting Logic

Create a scoring system for each category and occasion.
Example: Final Sort Score = Performance Score + Seasonal Boost + Manual Boost - Inventory Penalty

The score can consider:
Demand
Page views
Clicks
Search volume
Add-to-cart activity
Conversion
Conversion rate after clicking the category/occasion
Add-to-cart rate
Purchase completion rate
Revenue
Total sales generated by that category/occasion
Average order value
Margin
Prioritize higher-margin categories when performance is similar
Product Availability
Penalize categories/occasions with very few active products
Hide or deprioritize categories with no sellable products
City Relevance
Sorting should be city-specific where possible
Beirut, Dubai, and other cities may have different customer behavior
Seasonality
Boost relevant occasions during seasonal moments
Examples:
Valentine’s season: Love & Romance moves up
Mother’s Day: Mother’s Day moves up
Graduation season: Graduation moves up
Christmas/New Year: holiday gifting moves up
Manual Business Boost
Admin team should be able to manually boost certain categories or occasions
Example: Boost Lux Arrangements if we want to position Presentail as more premium

Suggested Default Order for Occasions
For normal periods, occasions should likely appear in this order:
Birthday
Love & Romance
Thank You
Get Well Soon
Anniversary
Congratulations
Graduation
New Baby
Sympathy
I’m Sorry
Birthday should usually remain near the front because it is an evergreen, high-intent occasion.

Suggested Default Order for Categories
For normal periods, categories can start with:
Flower Bouquets
Flower Boxes
Lux Arrangements
Gift Baskets
Flower Vases
Flower Baskets
Preserved Flowers
Plants
Cakes
Balloons / Add-ons
However, categories should not be fully AI-controlled because they also affect brand positioning. We should allow manual overrides.

Important UX Rule
The first 5–7 items in each row are the most important because they are visible before horizontal scrolling.
The sorting should optimize for:
What customers are most likely to click
What customers are most likely to buy
What has enough product depth
What supports Presentail’s brand positioning
What is relevant to the customer’s city and season

Requirements
Please explore and propose how to implement this inside Presentail OS and the customer website.
The system should include:
AI/performance-based sorting for categories and occasions
City-specific ranking
Seasonal boost logic
Product-count and availability checks
Manual admin boost/override
Fallback default order when there is not enough data
Ability to explain why an item is ranked higher or lower
Separate handling for categories vs occasions:
Occasions can be more dynamic
Categories should be semi-manual because of brand positioning

Acceptance Criteria
Homepage category and occasion rows are no longer randomly or alphabetically sorted.
Items with higher conversion opportunity appear earlier.
Items with no active products are hidden or moved to the end.
Admin can manually boost or pin important categories/occasions.
Seasonal occasions can automatically move up during relevant periods.
Sorting can differ by city.
The system has a safe fallback order if not enough data exists.
The first visible row on mobile shows the most commercially important items.
