OBJECTIVE
Make the midnight-delivery message immediately understandable when a customer selects today's 11 PM–1 AM delivery window.

LOCATION
- Surface: Product detail page → delivery date/time selector
- Example page: https://presentail.com/en-lb/beirut/product/plum-florals
- Component: “Midnight Delivery” informational card shown beneath the 11 PM–1 AM time-slot option
- Current example state: Today, Thu 20 Aug selected; 11 PM–1 AM selected; the window ends Fri, 21 Aug

CURRENT COPY
“Starts between 11 PM the day before and 1 AM on Fri, 21 Aug”

REQUIRED COPY FOR TODAY
“Arrives between 11 PM tonight and 1 AM on Fri, 21 Aug”

IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
1. Replace “Starts” with “Arrives” because the displayed time is the customer’s delivery/arrival window.
2. When the selected delivery date is the storefront’s current local date, display “tonight.”
3. The date after 1 AM must be calculated dynamically as the calendar day after the selected delivery date.
4. Do not hardcode “Fri, 21 Aug” or any weekday/date.
5. Use the storefront/delivery market timezone for “today” and “tonight,” not the customer device timezone. For the Lebanon storefront, this should follow Lebanon local time.
6. When the selected date is not today, do not use “tonight.” Use explicit start and end dates to avoid ambiguity:
   “Arrives between 11 PM on [start weekday/date] and 1 AM on [end weekday/date]”
7. Keep the displayed date format consistent with the rest of the delivery selector.
8. Apply the wording consistently anywhere this midnight-window helper text is reused across web and app, if both surfaces share the same component or translation key.
9. Update the English copy/translation key only; do not alter pricing, eligibility, slot availability, or delivery-fee logic.
10. Ensure wrapping remains clean on supported desktop and mobile widths and does not collide with the “+AED 75” price.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
- Given today is Thu, 20 Aug and the customer selects Today + 11 PM–1 AM, the card reads exactly:
  “Arrives between 11 PM tonight and 1 AM on Fri, 21 Aug”
- “the day before” no longer appears in the today-selected state.
- Given the customer selects a future date, “tonight” is not shown; both the 11 PM start date and next-day 1 AM end date are explicit.
- The end date advances correctly across:
  - weekday changes
  - month end (e.g. 31 Aug → 1 Sep)
  - year end (e.g. 31 Dec → 1 Jan)
  - leap day where applicable
- Day name and date remain correct for the storefront timezone around midnight.
- Existing behavior for other time slots is unchanged.
- Midnight-delivery fee, availability rules, selection state, checkout summary, and add-to-cart behavior are unchanged.
- Copy is readable and correctly wrapped on desktop and mobile.
- No regression occurs on Lebanon or other markets offering the 11 PM–1 AM slot.

8. Confirm all other delivery windows and fees behave exactly as before.
