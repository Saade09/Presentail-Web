Mobile Cart: Improve product card hierarchy and controls
This task is visible to its collaborators.
Mobile Cart: Improve product card hierarchy and controls
Assignee
Saade
Due date
No due date
Dependencies
Projects
Description
Objective



Improve the product card on the mobile cart so the product image, name, price, quantity, and removal action are immediately clear and easy to use.



Approved design direction



Redesign each cart product card using the approved mobile mockup:



- Keep the card white with the existing rounded corners, subtle border, and restrained shadow.

- Increase the product image significantly so the customer can clearly verify what they are purchasing.

- Use a square image area with rounded corners and preserve the original product-image aspect ratio without distortion.

- Group the product name and price together on the right side:

  - Product name first, using the existing product-title styling.

  - Price directly below in bold Presentail teal.

- Add a small “Quantity” label above the quantity selector.

- Increase the size of the quantity stepper and its tap targets.

- Show a clear “Remove” text action with a trash icon below the quantity control.

- Keep the Remove action away from the card edge and visually secondary to quantity changes.

- Allow the card to become slightly taller to achieve comfortable spacing.



Content rules



- Product name, image, unit price, quantity, and currency must be populated dynamically.

- Do not add generic variant or product-detail text when no such data exists.

- If a product has a customer-selected option that already appears in the cart, preserve that information without crowding the core hierarchy.

- Prices must follow the selected currency and existing Presentail price-formatting rules.



Quantity interaction



- The minus and plus controls must each have a minimum 44 × 44 px tap target.

- Tapping plus or minus must update:

  - Item quantity

  - Item price where applicable

  - Cart total

  - Free-delivery progress

  - Sticky checkout total

- Prevent duplicate rapid updates while a quantity request is processing.

- Show a lightweight loading or disabled state during updates.

- At quantity 1, tapping minus must follow the product’s existing removal behavior; do not create an inconsistent second flow.

- If an item cannot be increased due to stock limits, disable plus and show the existing inventory message.



Remove interaction



- Tapping “Remove” must use the existing cart-removal behavior.

- Ensure removal updates the cart count, totals, delivery threshold, recommendations, and sticky checkout bar.

- Keep the action accessible to keyboard and screen-reader users.

- Avoid relying on the trash icon alone; the “Remove” label must remain visible.



Mobile layout



- Apply the redesign to mobile cart product cards.

- Maintain consistent card padding and spacing across all products.

- Support multiple cart items without overlap or inconsistent card heights.

- Long product names may wrap to two lines while keeping the price and controls aligned.

- Ensure the layout works on narrow mobile screens and at increased text sizes.

- Preserve sufficient spacing between the product card, free-delivery progress, and card-message section.



Visual requirements



- Match the current Presentail design system and approved mockup.

- Use the existing white, pale gray, and dark teal palette.

- Keep shadows subtle and avoid gradients or heavy borders.

- Product imagery should remain the strongest visual element.

- Do not duplicate the product price in multiple places inside the card.

- Preserve the existing page header, promo-code control, delivery-progress component, card-message section, and sticky checkout component.



Accessibility



- Maintain WCAG-compliant text and control contrast.

- Provide accessible labels for decrease quantity, increase quantity, and remove product.

- Ensure the visible focus state is clear.

- Do not use color alone to communicate disabled or loading states.



Acceptance criteria



- Product image is visibly larger and remains sharp and undistorted.

- Product name and price appear together with clear hierarchy.

- Quantity label and stepper are easy to scan and tap.

- Remove is displayed as an icon plus text and is separated from the card edge.

- Quantity changes update all dependent cart totals and delivery-threshold messaging in real time.

- Loading, stock-limit, minimum-quantity, and removal states work correctly.

- Multiple product cards render consistently.

- Long names and English, Arabic, and French text do not break the layout.

- Desktop and unrelated cart components remain unchanged.

- Test on supported iPhone and Android viewport sizes.
