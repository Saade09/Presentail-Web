OBJECTIVE

Make the Express-delivery upsell context-aware so Presentail increases same-day conversion without accidentally encouraging gifts to arrive before the customer’s intended occasion date.

CORE PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

The gating decision must be based on the source of the selected delivery date—not simply whether the selected date is tomorrow or several days away.

If the customer explicitly chose a future date/time, treat that as deliberate gifting intent and do not proactively upsell delivery today.

If the system automatically assigned a future Standard slot because it was the earliest/default option, an earlier Express option may be useful, but it must be presented as a date change rather than an ordinary speed upgrade.

DEFINITIONS / STATE TO TRACK

Persist the source of the active delivery selection in cart/checkout state:

- system_default: automatically selected by Presentail
- user_selected: customer explicitly chose or confirmed the date/window
- restored_user_selection: a previously explicit choice restored from session/cart
- system_reselected: system changed the slot because the prior slot became unavailable

Do not infer intent solely from the selected date. The source should survive refresh, navigation, login, and cart restoration where possible.

DECISION LOGIC

1. STANDARD DELIVERY SELECTED FOR TODAY

When all cart items and the destination are Express-eligible:
- Show the full Express upgrade card directly under selected Standard delivery.
- Make the entire card clickable as one accessible control.
- Do not implement a clickable card containing a nested button; use a single button/radio-style control with one focus target.
- Show:
  - “Express delivery”
  - “Arrives by [exact local time]”
  - “Within 90 minutes”
  - The exact incremental price, e.g. “+ $10”
  - A visible “Upgrade” action
- Clicking it may apply Express directly because the calendar date is not changing.
- Update Delivery Summary, Order Summary, Total, and checkout CTA atomically.

2. FUTURE DELIVERY EXPLICITLY CHOSEN BY THE CUSTOMER

This applies equally to tomorrow, two days away, or any later date.

Examples:
- Customer manually selects tomorrow, 9 AM–2 PM.
- Customer selects a birthday date two weeks away.
- A restored cart contains a previously confirmed future delivery selection.

Behavior:
- Do not show the Express upsell card.
- Do not show “Need it sooner?” promotional copy.
- Show only the selected scheduled-delivery card and “Change”.
- Keep Express available inside the delivery selector opened by “Change”, if operationally eligible.
- Never auto-switch or one-click-switch the order to an earlier calendar date.

Reason:
For gifting, earlier delivery is not always better. Delivering before a birthday, anniversary, or event can be a fulfillment failure even when technically faster.

3. FUTURE DELIVERY AUTOMATICALLY ASSIGNED BY THE SYSTEM

Example:
Presentail automatically selects tomorrow morning because no Standard slot is available today and the customer has not manually confirmed a date.

When same-day Express is available:
- Do not use the normal “Upgrade” treatment.
- Show a quieter secondary prompt:
  - “Need it today?”
  - “Express arrives by [exact local time]”
  - Exact incremental price, e.g. “+ $15”
  - CTA: “See option”
- The entire prompt may be clickable using one accessible control.
- Clicking must open a review/confirmation step; it must not immediately change the delivery date.

If the customer dismisses the prompt or subsequently confirms the future slot, mark the choice as user-selected and suppress the proactive prompt for the remainder of that cart state unless delivery availability materially changes.

4. DELIVERY SELECTOR OPENED THROUGH “CHANGE”

List eligible delivery options with the selected option clearly marked.

Example:
- Today by 7:41 PM — +$15
- Tomorrow, 9 AM–2 PM — FREE · Selected

Express can appear here even when the proactive upsell is suppressed.

If the new option changes the delivery calendar date, require explicit confirmation before applying it.

DATE-CHANGE CONFIRMATION

Suggested copy:

Title:
“Deliver earlier?”

Body:
“Your delivery will change from tomorrow, 9 AM–2 PM to today by 7:41 PM for +$15.”

Actions:
- Primary: “Deliver earlier”
- Secondary: “Keep scheduled delivery”

Requirements:
- Include the complete original date/window.
- Include the complete new date/ETA.
- Show the exact change in total.
- Do not apply the new method until the primary action is confirmed.
- Cancel/close must preserve the original selection and total.
- Use destination timezone and locale-aware date/time formatting.

PRICING LOGIC

The amount presented beside Express must exactly equal the change that will appear in Total.

Calculate dynamically from:
- Current effective delivery charge
- Free-delivery benefits or delivery credits
- Express delivery fee
- Late-night, remote-area, holiday, or other delivery surcharges
- Any promotion whose eligibility changes with the delivery method

Do not hardcode the delta.

Examples:
- Current Standard $7, Express total $17: show +$10.
- Standard specifically waived and Express total $15: show +$15.
- Customer has a transferable $7 delivery credit and Express total $15: show +$8.

If the fee cannot be finalized before selection, show a review flow instead of an inaccurate delta.

ETA / DATE RULES

- “Arrives by [time]” must come from the live fulfillment promise, never a hardcoded client calculation.
- Compare actual destination-local calendar dates, not only elapsed hours.
- Recalculate after changes to address, market, products, quantity, inventory, promotions, fulfillment availability, or current time.
- If exact ETA becomes unavailable, remove the proactive prompt or fall back to a non-stale eligible option inside “Change”.
- A 90-minute ETA crossing midnight counts as a calendar-date change and requires confirmation.
- If the scheduled window is today and Express is also today, use the standard full upgrade card.
- If any item in the cart is not Express-eligible, suppress the cart-level upsell unless split fulfillment is explicitly supported.

UI / ACCESSIBILITY

- The complete Express card or prompt should be clickable, with one semantic interactive element.
- Provide visible hover, focus, pressed, loading, selected, disabled, and error states.
- Keyboard activation must work with Enter and Space.
- Screen-reader label must include method, arrival promise, and incremental price.
- Prevent duplicate requests from rapid clicks.
- During selection, retain layout and show a localized loading state.
- Do not let price, ETA, or CTA wrap awkwardly at supported mobile widths.
- After Express is selected, collapse to the compact selected-Express card with exact ETA and “Change”.

ORDER SUMMARY BEHAVIOR

- Show confirmed charges only.
- Before selection, do not add Express to Order Summary.
- After selection, show one delivery method row unless separate surcharges are genuinely additive and clearly labeled.
- Update Total and checkout CTA in the same committed state.
- Roll back all displayed values if the selection request fails.
- Never show Standard and Express as duplicate base-delivery charges.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- Full Express upgrade card appears when Standard and Express are both scheduled for today.
- Full Express upgrade card is entirely clickable through one accessible control.
- Explicitly selecting tomorrow suppresses the proactive Express upsell.
- Explicitly selecting any later future date also suppresses the proactive Express upsell.
- A future date automatically assigned by the system may show the quieter “Need it today?” prompt.
- The automatic-future prompt opens confirmation instead of immediately changing the delivery date.
- “Change” always exposes all eligible delivery options, even when the proactive upsell is hidden.
- Any calendar-date change requires confirmation with old date, new date, and exact total difference.
- Canceling confirmation leaves selection and pricing unchanged.
- The displayed delta always matches the resulting Total change.
- Destination timezone and locale formatting are correct.
- Selection intent survives refresh/cart restoration.
- Customer dismissal is respected and does not repeatedly reappear.
- Ineligible or mixed carts do not display an unusable offer.
- Order Summary contains only confirmed delivery charges.
- Mobile, keyboard, screen-reader, loading, error, and rapid-click behaviors pass QA.

REQUIRED TEST MATRIX

Cover at minimum:
- Today Standard → today Express
- Tomorrow explicitly selected → today Express available
- Tomorrow system-assigned → today Express available
- Two-plus-days explicitly selected
- Restored future user selection
- Express ETA crossing midnight
- Free Standard that does not transfer to Express
- Transferable delivery credit
- Late-night/remote-area surcharge
- Address/market change
- Item or quantity change causing Express ineligibility
- Exact ETA becoming unavailable
- Price request failure
- Confirmation cancel and confirm
- Refresh/back-navigation persistence
- Desktop and mobile responsive layouts
