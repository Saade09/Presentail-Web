
Objective
Reduce the vertical length of the Delivery Details checkout page on mobile so customers can move through the form faster and see more content per screen, while keeping the checkout comfortable, clear, and easy to tap.

Critical scope restriction
This change is for mobile only.
Apply the compact spacing at mobile breakpoints only, recommended at widths of 767 px and below.
Do not change the tablet or desktop checkout layout, spacing, field dimensions, typography, or card structure.


Design direction
Use the approved compact mobile mockup shared in the ChatGPT conversation. The page should feel modestly shorter and more efficient—not dense or cramped. Target approximately 12–15% less vertical page length compared with the current mobile implementation.

Required changes
Recipient Details card
Reduce the card’s top and bottom padding modestly.
Use consistent, tighter spacing between each field label and its input.
Reduce the vertical gap between form rows.
Preserve all existing fields, labels, validation, country selector, information icon, and “Ask the recipient for the address” control.
Delivery address field
Reduce the textarea height by approximately 25% on mobile.
Keep it visibly multiline and large enough for comfortable entry.
Preserve the placeholder exactly: “Street, building, floor, apartment”.
Do not change address validation, autocomplete, or saved-address behavior.
Delivery summary card
Reduce excess vertical padding around the delivery summary slightly.
Keep the delivery method, date, time window, supporting text, calendar icon, and “Change” action fully visible.
Do not change the delivery-slot logic or wording as part of this task.
Sender Details card
Reduce the card’s top and bottom padding modestly.
Tighten the vertical gaps between First Name/Last Name, Email Address, and WhatsApp Number.
Tighten the spacing around “Get order updates on WhatsApp” and “Send this gift anonymously” without reducing readability.
Keep First Name and Last Name side-by-side exactly as currently designed.
Preserve WhatsApp opt-in behavior, default state, anonymous-gift behavior, and all helper text.
Bottom of page
Remove unnecessary empty space between the end of the Sender Details card and the sticky checkout footer.
Preserve enough separation so the card does not visually collide with the footer.
Keep the sticky Back button, Continue to Payment button, order total, arrow, and Secure checkout treatment unchanged.


Do not change
Do not remove, merge, reorder, or hide any checkout section.
Do not change the sign-in card, its three sign-in options, or guest-checkout copy.
Do not change the Order Summary behavior or content.
Do not change field widths or the existing 16 px mobile page gutters.
Do not reduce body-text size.
Do not reduce interactive controls below a 44 px minimum touch target.
Do not change colors, typography, borders, corner radii, shadows, totals, copy, validation, or checkout functionality.
Do not apply these compact spacing values to tablet or desktop.


Implementation guidance
Use mobile-specific responsive spacing tokens or media-query rules rather than globally changing shared card/input styles.
Reuse one consistent mobile spacing system across Recipient Details, Delivery, and Sender Details.
Avoid one-off negative margins or absolute positioning.
Confirm the sticky footer does not cover the final anonymous-gift helper text at any supported mobile height.
Account for browser safe-area insets on iOS.


Acceptance criteria
The mobile Delivery Details page is visibly shorter by approximately 12–15% compared with the current version.
All existing content, sections, controls, copy, and functionality remain present.
The Delivery address field is approximately 25% shorter while remaining comfortably usable.
Spacing within Recipient Details and Sender Details is tighter and visually consistent.
There is no excessive blank space above the sticky checkout footer.
All mobile inputs and controls retain a minimum 44 px touch target.
The sticky footer never overlaps or hides the final form content.
No horizontal scrolling, clipping, broken wrapping, or unexpected layout shift occurs.
Tablet and desktop checkout remain pixel-for-pixel unchanged.
The result works in English, Arabic/RTL, and French without clipped labels or helper text.
