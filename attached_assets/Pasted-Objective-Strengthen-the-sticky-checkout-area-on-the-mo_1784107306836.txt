Objective

Strengthen the sticky checkout area on the mobile cart so customers can immediately understand their order context, total, and next action without the bar feeling bulky.

Approved design direction

Replace the current single teal pill that combines “Proceed to Checkout” and the price with a compact white sticky bottom sheet:

- Add a summary row above the CTA.
- Left side: dynamically show the item count and selected delivery context, for example: “1 item · Express delivery today”.
- Right side: show the small label “Total” above the final amount, for example: “$116”.
- Below the summary row, show one large, full-width Presentail teal button.
- Inside the button, place a white lock icon followed by “Proceed to Checkout” on the left and a right-arrow on the far right.
- Do not repeat the total inside the button.
- Use a subtle upward shadow, rounded top corners, white background, and mobile safe-area padding.

Functional requirements

- Item count must be dynamic and use correct singular/plural wording: “1 item” or “2 items”.
- Delivery context must reflect the customer’s actual selection:
  - “Express delivery today”
  - “Standard delivery today”
  - Or the selected future delivery date when applicable.
- Total must use the active currency and update immediately when:
  - Product quantities change
  - Add-on products are added or removed
  - Promo codes are applied or removed
  - Delivery method or fee changes
- The CTA must continue to use the existing checkout action and routing.
- While checkout is loading, prevent duplicate taps and show a clear loading state.
- If checkout cannot proceed, retain the existing validation behavior and make the CTA disabled where appropriate.

Mobile behavior

- Apply this redesign to the mobile cart only.
- Keep the component fixed to the bottom of the viewport while scrolling.
- Respect iOS and Android safe-area insets.
- Ensure the sticky component does not cover the final cart content or footer; add equivalent bottom spacing to the page.
- Keep the button at least 48px high with comfortable tap targets.
- Maintain the correct z-index above page content, menus, and product cards without covering active modals or drawers.

Visual styling

- Match the existing Presentail design system.
- Use the existing dark teal brand color for the CTA and price.
- Use dark gray for the order-context line and muted gray for the “Total” label.
- Keep shadows subtle and avoid gradients or heavy borders.
- Preserve the current typography and spacing system.
- The component should feel premium, compact, and clearly separated from the page content.

Acceptance criteria

- The sticky checkout component remains visible throughout the mobile cart journey.
- The item count, delivery context, currency, and total are accurate and update in real time.
- The amount is displayed once in the summary area and is not duplicated inside the CTA.
- Tapping “Proceed to Checkout” follows the current checkout flow.
- Loading and disabled states prevent duplicate checkout attempts.
- The bar does not obscure page content on supported mobile screen sizes.
- Safe-area spacing works correctly on iPhones with a home indicator.
- Desktop and tablet layouts remain unchanged.
- Verify the design with 1 item, multiple items, standard delivery, express delivery, future-date delivery, promo-code changes, and long translated text in English, Arabic, and French.
