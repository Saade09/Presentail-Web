Objective
Improve the mobile checkout payment page so customers can confirm the order and select a payment method more quickly, with less vertical scrolling and a clearer sense of progress.

Scope
Implement the approved mobile checkout refinements shown in the final mockup. Preserve the existing checkout logic, payment integrations, CTA copy/behavior, order-instructions module, and delivery-details module.

1. Selected payment-method state
- Apply a subtle pale-mint background to the selected payment row.
- Use a crisp 2 px dark Presentail-teal border.
- Keep the selected radio control filled in teal and clearly visible.
- Make the full payment row tappable.
- Do not add “Fastest,” “Recommended,” or any other promotional badge.
- Unselected rows should retain the neutral white background and light border.

2. Compact payment options
- Reduce each payment-method row to approximately 60–64 px high where content permits.
- Maintain a minimum 48 px interactive touch target.
- Reduce vertical gaps between payment rows to approximately 8–10 px.
- Reduce the payment card’s internal padding to approximately 16–20 px.
- Keep radio buttons, labels, and payment logos vertically centered.
- Align payment logos consistently on the right.
- Slightly reduce oversized logo tiles where necessary, without reducing legibility.
- The compact layout should move the existing payment CTA higher on the screen.

3. Essential collapsed order recap
- Keep Order Summary collapsed by default and preserve the existing expand/collapse behavior.
- Use a two-line layout on the left:
  - Primary: “Order Summary”
  - Secondary dynamic recap: “[item count] item/items · [delivery status]”
- Keep the formatted order total and downward chevron on the right, vertically centered against the two-line content.
- Delivery-status logic:
  - Free delivery: “3 items · Free delivery”
  - Paid delivery: “3 items · Delivery $9” using the active checkout currency and localized formatting
  - Singular: “1 item · Free delivery”
- Keep individual products, discounts, taxes, and other calculations inside the expanded summary.
- Make the entire summary row tappable and expose its expanded/collapsed state accessibly.

4. Compact progress header
- Reduce the mobile checkout header height by approximately 25%.
- Keep a slightly smaller Presentail wordmark on the left.
- Replace the vertically stacked stepper with one compact horizontal progress row:
  - Completed white circle with teal checkmark + “Delivery”
  - Short connector line
  - Active white circle with teal “2” + “Payment”
- Use approximately 32–36 px circles.
- Keep Payment bright white and semibold as the active step.
- Keep Delivery slightly muted but fully readable.
- Ensure the header fits on one line without wrapping or clipping on supported mobile widths.

Responsive and localization requirements
- Apply the approved compact treatment to mobile checkout.
- Do not introduce regressions on tablet or desktop.
- Verify English, Arabic/RTL, and French layouts.
- Allow safe responsive spacing for longer localized labels rather than clipping text.
- Preserve current multi-currency formatting.

Accessibility
- Maintain at least 48 × 48 px touch targets for payment choices and expandable controls.
- Preserve keyboard navigation and visible focus states.
- Ensure selected payment methods remain identifiable without relying only on background color.
- Use appropriate radio-group semantics and aria-expanded on Order Summary.
- Maintain sufficient text and border contrast.

Acceptance criteria
- The payment CTA appears materially higher on the initial mobile viewport.
- Payment options remain easy to tap and all payment provider logos remain legible.
- Only one payment method can be selected at a time.
- Selected state uses mint background, teal border, and filled radio control with no badge.
- Collapsed Order Summary displays item count, delivery cost/status, total, and chevron.
- Item singular/plural and paid/free delivery states render correctly.
- Progress header is approximately 25% shorter and remains readable on common mobile widths.
- Existing payment processing, order totals, CTA behavior, order instructions, and delivery details continue to work unchanged.

QA scenarios
- Apple Pay, card, PayPal, and Whish Money selected individually.
- One item versus multiple items.
- Free delivery versus paid delivery.
- USD, AED, EUR, and LBP totals, including long formatted amounts.
- English, French, and Arabic/RTL.
- Small mobile widths and larger mobile widths.
- Expanded and collapsed Order Summary.
- Keyboard and screen-reader interaction.
- Verify no visual or functional regressions on desktop checkout.
