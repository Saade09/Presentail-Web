Update the reassurance section on the mobile product page to reduce its vertical footprint while keeping all three benefits visible.
Current issue
The three reassurance messages are displayed as separate full-width cards, making the product page unnecessarily long and pushing payment information and product details farther down.
Required design
Replace the three stacked cards with one compact, full-width card containing three equal columns:
Free Standard Delivery
Helper text: Above €80
We Collect the Address
Helper text: If needed
Live Order Tracking
Helper text: Real-time updates
Each column should include:
The existing icon inside a light-grey circular background
A short, bold heading
Smaller muted helper text
Centered alignment
A subtle vertical divider between columns
Dynamic requirements
The free-delivery threshold must be loaded dynamically based on the selected city, currency and applicable delivery rules.
Do not hardcode €80.
The threshold should update when the user changes their city or currency.
If free standard delivery is unavailable for a city, hide the first benefit and redistribute the remaining columns evenly.
Visual requirements
Preserve Presentail’s existing colours, icons, border radius and card style.
Use one subtle border or shadow around the entire container.
Do not give each benefit its own card.
Keep text legible without making the section feel crowded.
The combined section should occupy approximately the height of one existing reassurance card.
Ensure each benefit remains tappable if it opens additional information.
Responsive behaviour
Mobile: three equal columns in one row.
Desktop: maintain the compact horizontal layout and align it with the product information area.
Prevent headings from overflowing or breaking into more than two lines.
Confirm that the layout works correctly in English, Arabic and French.
In Arabic, reverse the column order and apply RTL alignment where appropriate.
Acceptance criteria
The three stacked reassurance cards are replaced by one compact container.
All three benefits remain immediately visible.
The free-delivery threshold is dynamic.
The section works across supported screen sizes and languages.
Payment methods and product information appear higher on the page.
No changes are made to the delivery selector or other product-page sections.
