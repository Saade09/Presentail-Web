## Where this page already stands (better than our first two audits)

The per-occasion template fix that shipped after our earlier conversations is live here too. Current state:

```
Title: Mothers Day Flowers & Gifts in Beirut | Presentail
Meta:  Send Mothers Day flowers, cakes, balloons and gifts in Beirut. Order
       online with Presentail for express same-day delivery.
```

That's occasion-specific and city-specific — a real improvement over the fully generic template we found on the birthday page originally. I checked Tripoli, Zahle, Jbeil, and Saida's Mother's Day pages too, and they all follow this same formulaic pattern (`"{Occasion} Flowers & Gifts in {City} | Presentail"`), just with the city swapped — so this is a shared, auto-generated template applied consistently across every Lebanese city, not hand-written unique content per page yet.

## What's still missing

- **No apostrophe.** "Mothers Day" should be "Mother's Day" — small thing, but it's user-facing brand copy, not just a slug, and it reads as a typo. Easy fix.
- **No "Lebanon" anywhere.** You gave me the keyword as "Mother's Day Gifts Lebanon" — a national search, not "...Beirut." Same situation as the cakes page: this page currently only targets the local variant.
- **H1 is still hidden.** Raw HTML shows `<h1 class="sr-only">Mothers Day Flowers & Gifts in Beirut | Presentail</h1>` — visually hidden, and identical to the title tag rather than being its own heading.
- **No body copy, no FAQ, no BreadcrumbList/FAQPage schema.** Only the sitewide Organization schema is present. Same gap as birthday had before its content pass, and the same gap the cakes page still has.
- **Not in the sitemap.** Checked `sitemap-en.xml` again — still zero `occasion/` entries, consistent with the earlier sitemap audit (that fix doesn't appear to have shipped yet, or at least hasn't reached this environment).

## Competitive benchmark

[GiftBlooms' Beirut Mother's Day page](https://www.giftblooms.com/Lebanon/Mothers-Day-Gifts-to-Beirut) is the most directly useful comparison — it explicitly dual-targets the way I'm about to recommend:

- Title: *"Online Mother's Day Gifts Delivery to Beirut Lebanon | Unique Gifts for Mom"*
- H1: *"Send Mother's Day Gifts to Beirut Lebanon"* — city and country in the same heading, not split across separate pages
- ~1,200-1,400 words, several H2 sections, an FAQ block that includes *"Where can you deliver Mother's Day Gifts in Beirut, Lebanon?"* with an answer naming surrounding towns
- A "You may also like" cross-link section

[GiftBlooms also runs hyper-local pages](https://www.giftblooms.com/Lebanon/Mothers-Day-Gifts-to-Metn-District) for individual districts (Metn, Saida, Koura) as a separate long-tail strategy — worth knowing about, but not something I'd copy here; you already have proper city pages for this via your city-page architecture, so a district-page strategy would be redundant with what you're building.

## Recommended fix

Same underlying approach as the cakes page: **make Beirut's Mother's Day page the dual-purpose flagship** for "mother's day gifts beirut" and "mother's day gifts lebanon" together, rather than building a 26th national page. Beirut is the capital, the highest-volume market, and your Organization schema already declares `areaServed: ["LB","AE","CY"]`.

**Title:** `Mother's Day Gifts in Beirut, Lebanon | Same-Day | Presentail`

**Meta description:** `Send Mother's Day flowers, cakes and gift hampers in Beirut with same-day delivery — or send one anywhere else in Lebanon. Order online today.`

**H1 (visible, not sr-only):** `Mother's Day Gifts in Beirut, Lebanon`

**Body copy (~450-550 words):**
- Intro paragraph: same-day Mother's Day delivery in Beirut (name neighborhoods) *and* delivery nationwide across Lebanon for anyone ordering from another city or from abroad for a mother in Lebanon — this sentence is what actually earns the national query.
- H2 "Shop Mother's Day Gifts" — flowers, cakes, hampers, personalised gifts, linked to existing category pages.
- H2 "Delivering Mother's Day Gifts Across Lebanon" — short paragraph + linked list of other city Mother's Day pages (Tripoli, Zahle, Jbeil, Saida, etc. — same hub-and-spoke pattern as the cakes page), only linking to cities with populated content.
- FAQ block:
  - *Can I get same-day Mother's Day gift delivery in Beirut?* — use your confirmed Beirut cutoff.
  - *Do you deliver Mother's Day gifts outside Beirut, anywhere in Lebanon?*
  - *Can I order a Mother's Day gift from abroad for my mother in Lebanon?* — targets diaspora searchers specifically.
  - *What Mother's Day gifts are available?*
  - *Can I add a personalised message?*

**Structured data:** `BreadcrumbList` (Home > Occasions > Mother's Day) and `FAQPage`, matching the pattern from the birthday and cakes plans.

**Scope carefully:** apply this rich content only to Beirut's Mother's Day page — leave Tripoli, Zahle, Jbeil, Saida, and the rest on the current shared template so they keep owning their own local variant without competing with Beirut for "Lebanon."
