OBJECTIVE

Make the checkout delivery copy feel more natural by displaying “tonight” instead of “today” when the selected delivery window is later on the current day.

APPROVED EXAMPLE

- Delivery Summary: Arrives tonight, 9 PM–11 PM
- Sticky checkout bar: 1 item · Standard delivery tonight
- Keep Express Delivery copy unchanged: Arrives by [time]

IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

1. Determine “today” using the delivery market/location timezone, not the customer device timezone. This is important for customers ordering internationally and for Lebanon/UAE markets.
2. When the selected delivery date is today and the scheduled window begins at 6:00 PM or later, use “tonight.”
3. When the selected delivery date is today and the window begins before 6:00 PM, retain the existing “today” wording.
4. Apply the same dynamic label everywhere the delivery promise is summarized, including the Delivery Summary card and the mobile sticky checkout bar.
5. Do not hardcode the text separately in each component. Use shared delivery-date formatting/helper logic so both surfaces always stay consistent.
6. Preserve existing formatting, typography, spacing, pricing, icons, Change/Upgrade actions, and checkout behavior. This is a copy/logic update only.
7. Do not change future-date, “tomorrow,” Express Delivery, or Midnight Delivery service-specific copy. Cross-midnight windows must continue using the established Midnight Delivery wording rather than inheriting this rule blindly.
8. Use localized translation keys for English, Arabic, and French rather than embedding English directly in the UI.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- A same-day 9 PM–11 PM standard window displays “Arrives tonight, 9 PM–11 PM.”
- The sticky bar simultaneously displays “1 item · Standard delivery tonight.”
- A same-day daytime window continues to display “today.”
- A future delivery date never displays “tonight.”
- Express Delivery continues to display “Arrives by [time]” and “Within 90 minutes.”
- The result is consistent on mobile and desktop and across cart/checkout states where the delivery summary is reused.
- No layout wrapping, clipping, overlap, or change to the sticky checkout bar height on supported mobile widths.

QA SCENARIOS

- Window starts at 5:59 PM: today.
- Window starts at 6:00 PM: tonight.
- Window starts at 9:00 PM: tonight.
- Customer device timezone differs from the selected delivery market timezone: wording follows the delivery market.
- User changes the delivery date or district: both summary locations update immediately and remain synchronized.
- Check English, Arabic, and French layouts for correct translation and no truncation.
