Objective
Add the compact Klarna installment message directly beneath the product price on Presentail product pages, matching the approved mockup.

Scope — only implement this change
- Add a small Klarna payment message between the product price and the delivery-information section.
- Do not redesign the product image, product title, price, delivery section, Add to Cart button, product cards, or checkout.
- Do not add a second “Buy with Klarna” or express-checkout button.
- Do not add Klarna banners elsewhere on the website as part of this task.

Implementation requirements
- Use Stripe’s Payment Method Messaging Element or Stripe’s current supported dynamic messaging component. Do not hard-code “Pay in 4,” the installment amount, interest wording, or the Klarna country list.
- Allow Stripe to generate the localized, compliant offer using the current product amount, selected currency, locale, and customer eligibility.
- Show the Klarna message only when Stripe confirms that Klarna messaging is eligible for that customer country, presentment currency, product amount, and Stripe account.
- If Stripe does not return an eligible Klarna offer—or the customer location is unknown/ineligible—render nothing and preserve the existing spacing.
- Eligibility must use the payer/sender’s country or Stripe’s customer-location determination, not the flower recipient’s delivery country. Example: an eligible UK customer sending flowers to Lebanon or the UAE may see Klarna; a customer located in an unsupported market must not see it merely because the delivery destination is supported.
- The component must refresh when product price, variant, add-on total, quantity, or selected currency changes.
- Use Klarna’s official pink badge/logo supplied through the approved Stripe component. Keep the presentation compact and visually secondary to the Add to Cart button.
- “Learn more” must open the localized Klarna/Stripe explanation supplied by the component.
- Load the payment-messaging component without blocking the product page. A loading failure must not affect product purchase or create empty gaps.
- Support English, Arabic, and French, including correct RTL layout in Arabic.

Required visual placement
1. Product name
2. Price
3. Klarna dynamic payment message
4. Delivery information
5. Add to Cart

Acceptance criteria
- Eligible supported-country customer: sees the Stripe-generated Klarna message beneath the price.
- Unsupported-country customer: sees no Klarna message and no blank space.
- Supported country with unsupported currency or amount: sees no Klarna message.
- Changing currency or product total immediately re-evaluates and updates/hides the offer.
- Recipient/delivery country does not incorrectly control payer eligibility.
- Installment wording and amounts are never manually calculated or hard-coded.
- Existing product-page design and checkout remain unchanged.
- Verified on mobile and desktop in EN/AR/FR.
- QA includes at least one eligible market and one ineligible market using Stripe test mode.
