Objective

Make available and unavailable delivery areas immediately understandable. The current design heavily fades unavailable locations and repeats “Not available at the moment,” making the page feel unfinished and difficult to scan.

Approved Cyprus design

- Update the description to: “We currently deliver to Larnaca. More areas are coming soon.”
- Add an “Available now” section.
- Show Larnaca inside its own active location card with dark text and a right arrow.
- Add a separate “Coming soon” section below it.
- Show Limassol, Nicosia and Paphos inside a separate muted card.
- Use readable medium-grey text for unavailable areas.
- Remove arrows from unavailable rows to communicate that they are not interactive.
- Remove the repeated “Not available at the moment” text from each row.
- Ensure both cards dynamically fit their content without empty internal space.

Interaction requirements

- Available areas remain clickable and continue to the website.
- Coming-soon areas must not be clickable, focusable or selectable.
- Disabled rows must use the correct cursor and accessibility state.
- Screen readers should identify unavailable areas as unavailable.
- The structure should be data-driven and work for any country containing a mix of available and unavailable areas.

Design requirements

- Preserve the approved responsive heading and compact selected-country selector.
- Preserve Presentail’s typography, colors, borders, spacing and minimal visual style.
- Use subtle section labels matching the “Delivering to” label style.
- Keep disabled text readable and accessible; do not reduce opacity excessively.
- Do not add notification buttons or additional actions in this task.
- Ensure the layout works correctly on desktop and mobile.
- Apply translated copy across English, Arabic and French.

Acceptance criteria

- Cyprus displays Larnaca under “Available now.”
- Limassol, Nicosia and Paphos appear under “Coming soon.”
- Unavailable areas are clearly readable but visibly non-interactive.
- Repeated “Not available at the moment” copy is removed.
- Active and unavailable locations cannot be confused.
- Cards use content-driven height with no unnecessary empty space.
- The component supports countries with only available areas, only unavailable areas, or a mixture of both.
- No text overflow or layout regressions occur across supported languages and screen sizes.
