Please implement a clean SEO content section for category and occasion pages, based on the approved design direction.

Goal:
Add helpful visible SEO content at the bottom of category and occasion pages, after the product grid and before the footer. This should improve SEO without hurting the shopping experience.

Important:
Do not place SEO text above the products. Products should remain the priority. The SEO content should appear only after the product grid, before the footer.

Example page:
https://presentail.com/en-lb/beirut/category/hand-bouquets

Current structure:

* Header
* Breadcrumb
* H1: Hand Bouquets / 57 Products
* Sidebar categories / occasions
* Product grid
* Footer

New structure:

* Header
* Breadcrumb
* H1: Hand Bouquets / 57 Products
* Sidebar categories / occasions
* Product grid
* SEO content section
* Footer

Please create a reusable component called something like:
SEOContentSection or SEOContentBlock

This component should work for:

* Category pages
* Occasion pages

Do not add it yet to:

* Product pages
* Cart
* Checkout
* Login
* Account
* Search pages
* Noindex pages

1. Placement

On category and occasion pages, render the SEO content section:

* after the final product row
* before the footer
* inside the main page layout
* aligned visually with the product grid/container
* with enough spacing above and below

The section should not appear if:

* the page has no products
* the page is noindex
* the page is cart/checkout/account/login/search
* SEO content is disabled for that page type

2. Visual design

Use the same luxury/minimal Presentail style.

Design requirements:

* White or very light warm background
* Rounded card/container
* Subtle border or very soft shadow
* Generous padding
* Max width aligned with the product content area
* Clean typography
* H2 in the same elegant serif style as the website headings
* Body text in readable sans-serif
* Do not make the section look like a blog article
* Do not make it look spammy
* Keep it elegant and quiet

Suggested layout:

Top:

* H2 title
* 1 short intro paragraph

Middle:

* 3 or 4 small benefit items with simple icons

Below:

* “Popular occasions in {City}” or “Related categories”
* Internal link chips/buttons

Bottom:

* FAQ accordion with 3 questions

Example layout:

Paragraph:

3. Dynamic content templates

Please do not hardcode only “Hand Bouquets”. Build dynamic templates based on page type, city, language, category, and occasion.

For category pages:

H2 format:
{Category Name} Delivery in {City}

Intro paragraph format:
Shop {category name} online in {city} with Presentail. Choose from fresh flowers, plants, cakes, balloons, chocolates and gifts, with reliable delivery options available across {city}.

For flower-specific categories, use a more natural version:
Shop {category name} online in {city} with Presentail. Choose from fresh flower arrangements for birthdays, anniversaries, thank-you gifts, romantic occasions and more, with reliable delivery options across {city}.

For occasion pages:

H2 format:
Send {Occasion Name} Flowers & Gifts in {City}

Intro paragraph format:
Celebrate {occasion name} in {city} with flowers, cakes, balloons and thoughtful gifts from Presentail. Order online and choose the delivery option that works best for your recipient.

Examples:

* Hand Bouquet Delivery in Beirut
* Plants Delivery in Beirut
* Cakes Delivery in Beirut
* Send Birthday Flowers & Gifts in Beirut
* Send Condolences Flowers & Gifts in Beirut

4. Benefit cards

Add 3–4 reusable benefit items.

For category pages, default benefits:

* Fresh & Beautiful Flowers
* Same-day & Scheduled Delivery
* Recipient Address Collection
* Elegant Wrapping & Presentation

For non-flower categories like Cakes or Chocolates, adjust wording:

* Curated Gifts
* Same-day & Scheduled Delivery
* Recipient Address Collection
* Elegant Presentation

For occasion pages, default benefits:

* Thoughtful Gifts for Every Occasion
* Same-day & Scheduled Delivery
* Recipient Address Collection
* Flowers, Cakes & More

Keep icons simple and consistent with the existing website style. Use subtle outline icons.

5. Internal links

Add a row of internal link chips.

For category pages:
Title:
Popular occasions in {City}

Suggested links:

* Birthday Flowers
* Anniversary Flowers
* Love & Romance
* Thank You Flowers
* Congratulations

For occasion pages:
Title:
Popular gifts for {Occasion Name}

Suggested links:

* Flower Bouquets
* Flower Boxes
* Cakes
* Chocolates
* Plants

Rules:

* Links should use canonical city/language URLs.
* Do not hardcode Beirut only.
* Use the current selected city and language.
* Do not show broken links.
* If a linked category or occasion does not exist, hide that chip.
* Keep link chips subtle and not too visually dominant.

6. FAQ accordion

Add 3 FAQ questions per page.

For category pages:

Q1:
Can I send {category name} in {city}?
A:
Yes, you can order {category name} online and send them across {city} with Presentail.

Q2:
Does Presentail offer same-day delivery in {city}?
A:
Available delivery options depend on the selected product, date, time and city.

Q3:
Can Presentail ask the recipient for the address?
A:
Yes, Presentail can collect the recipient’s address if you do not know it.

For occasion pages:

Q1:
Can I send {occasion name} gifts in {city}?
A:
Yes, you can send flowers, cakes, balloons and gifts for {occasion name} across {city} with Presentail.

Q2:
Can I schedule delivery for {occasion name}?
A:
Yes, available scheduled delivery options are shown during checkout based on the selected city, date and product.

Q3:
What can I send for {occasion name}?
A:
You can choose from flowers, cakes, plants, chocolates, balloons and curated gifts depending on availability in your city.

FAQ behavior:

* Use accordion style.
* Keep first FAQ closed by default unless there is already a site convention.
* Make the whole row clickable.
* Use plus/minus icon.
* Maintain accessibility with proper button/aria-expanded attributes.

7. FAQ schema

Only add FAQPage structured data if the FAQs are visibly rendered on the page.

Rules:

* If FAQ accordion appears, add matching FAQPage JSON-LD.
* If FAQ section is hidden or disabled, do not add FAQPage schema.
* FAQ schema content must exactly match the visible questions and answers.

8. Multilingual support

Please prepare this component for English, French, and Arabic.

For this first implementation:

* English content must be clean and live.
* French and Arabic can use translated fallback templates if available.
* If translations are not ready, implement the structure so translation strings can be added easily.

Important:

* Arabic layout should support RTL.
* Do not show English SEO text on Arabic pages if the site already has Arabic translation infrastructure.
* Use the existing i18n system if available.

9. Mobile behavior

The section must look good on mobile.

Mobile requirements:

* Full-width section with comfortable padding
* Benefit items stack vertically or in 2-column grid
* Link chips wrap nicely
* FAQ rows are easy to tap
* No horizontal overflow
* Text size remains readable
* Footer spacing remains clean

10. Performance

The SEO section should not hurt page speed.

Rules:

* Do not load heavy images.
* Use lightweight icons.
* Avoid large JavaScript dependencies.
* Keep FAQ accordion simple.
* Make sure the section is server-rendered or present in initial HTML where possible so search engines can read it.

11. Admin/custom content future-proofing

Please structure the component so later we can replace fallback template text with custom SEO content from Presentail OS.

Suggested future data shape:

* page_type: category or occasion
* language
* country_code
* city_slug
* category_slug, optional
* occasion_slug, optional
* heading
* intro_text
* benefits
* internal_links
* faqs
* is_active

For now:

* Use dynamic fallback templates.
* Do not require admin CMS to exist before launching.

13. Acceptance criteria

This task is complete when:

* SEOContentBlock/SEOContentSection component exists.
* It appears below the product grid and above the footer on category pages.
* It appears below the product grid and above the footer on occasion pages.
* Products remain the focus of the page.
* H2 is dynamic based on category/occasion and city.
* Intro paragraph is dynamic.
* Benefit items render cleanly.
* Internal link chips point to valid canonical URLs.
* FAQ accordion works.
* FAQPage schema is added only when FAQs are visible.
* The design matches Presentail’s luxury/minimal style.
* Mobile layout works cleanly.
* No SEO text is added to cart, checkout, login, account, or search pages.
* No broken links are created.
* No product/category/occasion routing is changed.
