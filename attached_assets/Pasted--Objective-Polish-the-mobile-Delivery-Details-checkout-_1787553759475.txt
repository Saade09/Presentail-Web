
Objective
Polish the mobile Delivery Details checkout by correcting small copy, alignment, and component-style inconsistencies. This task should not redesign the page or change checkout behavior.

Scope
Mobile checkout only, at widths of 767 px and below.
Do not change tablet or desktop styling.
Apply the fixes to the Delivery Details checkout step shown in the approved screenshots.


Consistency checklist
Standardize all field labels to sentence case
“Recipient name” → “Recipient’s name”
“Phone Number” → “Phone number”
“Delivery District” → “Delivery district”
Keep “Delivery address” as written.
“First Name” → “First name”
“Last Name” → “Last name”
“Email Address” → “Email address”
“WhatsApp Number” → “WhatsApp number”
Section headings such as “Recipient Details” and “Sender Details” remain title case.
Standardize required-field asterisks
Use the same red color, size, weight, baseline alignment, and spacing after every required label.
Do not allow the asterisk to sit higher or farther away on specific fields.
Required status and validation logic must remain unchanged.
Refine the Phone number information icon
Reduce the visible icon slightly so it does not compete with the label; target approximately 16 px visually.
Vertically center it with the Phone number label text.
Use a consistent gap between the asterisk and information icon.
Preserve at least a 44 × 44 px invisible tap target for accessibility.
Do not change the approved contained-tooltip behavior or copy.
Standardize input styling
All text inputs, phone inputs, dropdowns, and the address textarea should use the same border color, border weight, shadow treatment, background color, and field-radius token.
The country-code selector and phone-number area should read as one unified input while retaining a clear internal divider.
Ensure input text and placeholder text use consistent font size, weight, vertical centering, and color.
Do not change field heights established by the separate mobile page-length optimization task.
Standardize interactive card styling
The three sign-in buttons should match one another exactly in height, border, radius, and shadow.
The “Ask the recipient for the address” control should use the same border color and visual weight as the rest of the checkout’s secondary controls.
The delivery summary should follow the same outer-card border and shadow treatment as the other checkout cards while retaining its light-blue inner summary state.
Do not force every component to have the same radius; use consistent tokens within each component family.
Standardize helper and secondary text
Use one consistent secondary-text color, font size, weight, and line height for “We’ll collect it by text,” the WhatsApp helper, and the anonymous-gift helper.
Keep explanatory copy visually subordinate to labels but readable.
Do not alter the meaning or behavior of any helper text.
Align icons and trailing controls
Vertically center country flags, dropdown chevrons, the address icon, toggle, calendar icon, “Change” link, checkboxes, and CTA arrow within their containers.
Use consistent icon sizes within comparable controls.
Confirm that icons do not shift vertically when text wraps.


Do not change
No sections, fields, buttons, helper copy, or functionality should be added, removed, merged, or reordered.
Do not change sign-in behavior, address-collection behavior, delivery-slot logic, validation, WhatsApp opt-in behavior, anonymous-gift behavior, or sticky-footer behavior.
Do not alter the checkout total, delivery wording, or existing responsive page-length work.
Do not make global design-token changes that unintentionally affect other pages.


Localization
Apply equivalent sentence-case and grammar improvements in English, French, and Arabic where linguistically appropriate.
Confirm the Arabic/RTL layout keeps required markers, information icons, flags, and chevrons aligned correctly.
Do not use a literal translation when it produces unnatural wording.


Acceptance criteria
All English form labels use the approved sentence-case wording.
“Recipient’s name” is displayed with the possessive apostrophe.
All required asterisks are visually identical and consistently aligned.
The Phone number information icon is visually smaller, centered with the label, and retains an accessible tap target.
Inputs within the same component family have consistent borders, radii, shadows, text treatment, and vertical alignment.
Sign-in buttons match one another exactly.
Helper text styling is consistent throughout the page.
Icons, chevrons, toggles, checkboxes, and trailing actions are centered and consistently sized.
No functional checkout behavior changes.
Tablet and desktop remain unchanged.
English, French, and Arabic mobile checkout pass visual QA without clipping or alignment regressions.
