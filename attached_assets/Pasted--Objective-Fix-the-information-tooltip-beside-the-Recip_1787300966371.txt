
Objective
Fix the information tooltip beside the Recipient Details “Phone Number *” label on mobile checkout so the complete message is readable without leaving the viewport or covering the phone input.

Current issue
On mobile, tapping the information icon opens a dark-teal tooltip that extends beyond the right edge of the screen.
The copy is clipped, so the customer cannot read the full explanation.
The overlay currently competes with the form and makes the checkout look broken.


Required design and behavior
Keep the existing information icon beside the “Phone Number *” label.
On tap, show a compact dark-teal rounded popover fully contained inside the Recipient Details card and inside the mobile viewport.
Use this exact copy: “We’ll only use this number for delivery coordination.”
Position the popover directly below the Phone Number label row and above the phone-number input.
Add a small upward pointer aligned toward the information icon.
Allow the sentence to wrap naturally onto a maximum of two lines.
Add sufficient vertical space while the popover is open so it does not overlap the label or input.
Do not change the phone input, country selector, validation logic, or any other checkout component.
Close the popover when the user taps the information icon again, taps outside the popover, presses Escape on a keyboard, or moves to another checkout step.
Only one informational popover may be open at a time.


Responsive requirements
The popover must remain within the Recipient Details card at all supported mobile widths, including narrow devices.
Use the available inner width of the card rather than a fixed width that can overflow.
Respect the card’s left and right content gutters.
On wider screens, keep the popover close to its trigger; do not stretch it unnecessarily across the entire form.
Account for English, Arabic, and French. The Arabic version must support RTL alignment and pointer placement without overflow.


Accessibility
The information icon must remain keyboard focusable and have an accessible label such as “Why we need the recipient’s phone number.”
Use an appropriate expanded state on the trigger.
Ensure tooltip text meets WCAG contrast requirements against the dark-teal background.
Do not rely solely on hover; it must work on tap, click, and keyboard activation.


Acceptance criteria
The full tooltip sentence is visible on mobile with no horizontal clipping or overflow.
The tooltip remains inside both the viewport and Recipient Details card.
The tooltip does not cover the Phone Number label or input.
The copy appears exactly as specified.
The tooltip opens and closes reliably through tap/click, outside tap, and keyboard interaction.
Opening the tooltip does not cause unwanted horizontal scrolling or layout shifting elsewhere on the page.
The behavior works in English, Arabic, and French.
No regression occurs in recipient phone entry, country-code selection, validation, sticky checkout footer, or desktop checkout.


QA devices/viewports
iPhone SE / narrow mobile width
Standard iPhone width
Large iPhone width
Android mobile width
Tablet and desktop checkout
