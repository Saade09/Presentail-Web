Objective
Add a premium Midnight Delivery option to the Presentail product-page delivery selector, following the approved mockup/design direction.

Customer-facing design
• Add a fifth delivery-time pill labeled “11 PM–1 AM”.
• Include a crescent-moon icon in the pill.
• Selected state: Presentail deep teal background with white icon/text.
• Unselected state: white background, light-gray border, dark text, consistent with existing time pills.
• When selected, show a pale champagne/cream premium panel beneath the time options:
  – “Midnight Delivery”
  – “+$20”
  – “Arrives as [selected date] begins”
• Update the delivery summary to show the selected occasion date and “11 PM–1 AM”.
• Preserve the existing Presentail typography, spacing, card styling and responsive mobile behavior.

Date behavior — critical
The customer selects the occasion date, meaning the calendar date that begins at midnight.
Example: Selecting Thu, 20 Aug means the operational delivery window is Wed, 19 Aug at 11:00 PM through Thu, 20 Aug at 1:00 AM.
The explanatory copy must read: “Arrives as Thu, 20 Aug begins.”
Ensure backend order timestamps, operations views and customer confirmations all use the correct cross-date window.

Pricing
• Midnight Delivery surcharge: $20.
• Show the surcharge beside the selected option and as a separate delivery line item in cart/checkout/order details.
• The existing free standard-delivery qualification must not remove or discount this surcharge.

Geographic availability
• Available ONLY for delivery addresses within Beirut and Metn.
• Use the existing canonical delivery-zone IDs/geofencing—not free-text city matching.
• Hide the midnight option when the known destination is outside Beirut or Metn.
• If the destination is not known yet, the option may be shown with eligibility checked when the address is selected.
• If a customer selects Midnight Delivery and subsequently changes the destination to an ineligible area, clear the slot, remove the $20 surcharge and require the customer to choose another delivery time.
• Server-side validation must prevent checkout with this slot outside Beirut and Metn, even if the client is bypassed.

Availability and operations
• Midnight slot must respect existing capacity, blackout dates, product availability and order cutoffs.
• Store the service as a distinct delivery method/slot so operations and reporting can identify it.
• Customer confirmation, internal order view and courier instructions must display the full cross-date delivery window clearly.

Analytics
Track at minimum:
• midnight_option_viewed
• midnight_option_selected
• midnight_option_ineligible
• midnight_selection_removed_after_address_change
• midnight_order_completed
Include area/zone, occasion date, product value and surcharge where appropriate.

Acceptance criteria
1. Eligible Beirut and Metn customers can select “11 PM–1 AM” and see the premium panel and +$20 surcharge.
2. Customers outside Beirut and Metn cannot complete checkout with Midnight Delivery.
3. Selecting an occasion date maps to the correct previous-day 11 PM through selected-day 1 AM operational window.
4. The $20 fee persists correctly through cart, checkout, payment, confirmation and internal order records.
5. Changing to an ineligible address removes the slot and surcharge without leaving stale order data.
6. The UI matches the approved mockup on common mobile widths and does not break the existing delivery selector.
7. Existing delivery methods, free-standard-delivery logic and totals continue to work unchanged.
8. QA covers Beirut, Metn, at least one ineligible area, address changes, month/year boundaries, sold-out capacity and timezone behavior.
