
Objective
Implement the approved compact delivery summary on the product detail page so customers can immediately understand the delivery method, arrival time/window, and whether the delivery fee is already included.

Design direction
Use one reusable compact delivery card for both Express and Scheduled delivery states.
Replace “DELIVERY FOR THIS ORDER” with the small uppercase label “DELIVERY”.
Use an extremely subtle warm-grey card background (approximately #F7F7F5), a thin pale-teal border, and the existing rounded-corner system.
Keep the card compact and position the existing Add to Cart button directly beneath it.
Preserve the rest of the PDP: title, price, rewards, CTA styling, reassurance tiles, payment methods, Trustpilot, and product imagery.


Remove from the current component
The “Selected in cart” badge.
The standalone “You qualify for free standard delivery” message above the card when an order-level delivery method is already selected.
Duplicate arrival-time, fee, and “already applied” explanations.
The internal divider and the long footer sentence.
“Change date or time”; use the shorter, consistent action label “Change”.


Express delivery state
Leading icon: teal circular lightning icon.
Title: Express delivery.
Right-aligned action: Change.
Primary detail: Arrives by {time} Lebanon time.
Secondary context: “This item will join your cart’s delivery.”
Fee line: “Express upgrade: {fee} · already included”.


Scheduled delivery state
Leading icon: teal circular calendar icon.
Title: Scheduled delivery.
Right-aligned action: Change.
Primary detail: {date} · {delivery window} Lebanon time. Example: “Today, Aug 13 · 9–11 PM Lebanon time”.
Secondary context: “This item will join your cart’s delivery.”
Fee line when free: “Delivery: Free · already included”.
If scheduled delivery is paid, replace “Free” with the actual order-level fee; never imply that the customer will be charged twice.


Content and data rules
All date, time, fee, and method values must come from the active cart/order-level delivery selection.
Always show “Lebanon time” for Lebanon delivery estimates and scheduled windows.
Use one arrival representation only: exact arrival time for Express; date plus window for Scheduled.
“Already included” means the fee is already present at order level and must not be added again when this product is added.
If the cart delivery selection changes, refresh the PDP card immediately.


Interaction
Selecting “Change” opens the existing delivery-method/date-time selection flow.
After a new method or window is confirmed, close the selector and update the compact card.
Adding the product preserves the active cart delivery selection.


Responsive and accessibility requirements
Keep the title and Change action on the first row at desktop and mobile widths.
Allow the date/window line to wrap cleanly on narrow screens without overlapping Change.
Maintain WCAG AA contrast for all text, link, border, and icon colors against #F7F7F5.
The full Change control must be keyboard accessible and have a descriptive accessible label.
Do not rely on the lightning/calendar icon alone to communicate the state.


Acceptance criteria
An Express cart displays the compact Express state with a dynamic arrival time and explicit “Lebanon time”.
A Scheduled cart displays the compact Scheduled state with the selected date and delivery window.
No “Selected in cart” badge, duplicate delivery promise, duplicate fee message, divider, or long footer remains.
The delivery card uses the approved warm-grey surface and remains visually subordinate to the Add to Cart CTA.
The delivery fee is displayed accurately and is not charged again when the item is added.
Change successfully updates the order-level selection and the PDP reflects the result without stale content.
The existing content outside the delivery module remains unchanged.
Desktop and mobile layouts pass visual QA for both delivery states.


QA scenarios
Express delivery with a paid upgrade already selected.
Scheduled delivery with a free delivery window already selected.
Scheduled delivery with a paid delivery window.
Change from Express to Scheduled and back.
Change the scheduled date/window and verify the PDP updates.
Add the product after each state and verify no duplicate delivery fee.
Verify time-zone wording and date formatting around midnight/day changes.
Verify long localized values wrap safely on mobile.


Out of scope
Redesigning the Add to Cart button, reassurance tiles, payment methods, Trustpilot block, rewards placement, or other PDP content.
