Build a free-delivery eligibility warning popup on Presentail.com using the attached design proposal as the visual reference.

OBJECTIVE

When a customer changes the delivery city, show a warning if the cart qualified for free delivery in the previous city but no longer qualifies in the newly selected city because its minimum free-delivery threshold is higher.

TRIGGER LOGIC

After the customer confirms a new delivery city:

1. Read the previous city’s free-delivery threshold.
2. Read the new city’s free-delivery threshold.
3. Recalculate eligibility using the same cart-subtotal and delivery-fee logic used by checkout.
4. Show the popup only when:
   - The cart qualified for free delivery before the city change.
   - The cart no longer qualifies after the city change.
5. Do not show it when:
   - The cart remains eligible.
   - The cart was already ineligible.
   - The cart is empty.
   - No free-delivery threshold applies.
6. Do not hardcode cities, thresholds, currencies or delivery rules. Use the existing backend delivery configuration.
7. Keep the newly selected city active even if the customer closes the popup.

POPUP CONTENT

Heading:

“Free delivery has changed”

Body:

“The free delivery minimum is different in [NEW CITY]. Your cart no longer qualifies for free delivery.”

Show a bordered summary box containing:

Cart subtotal                         [FORMATTED CART SUBTOTAL]
Free delivery from                   [FORMATTED NEW THRESHOLD]

Add a progress bar calculated as:

qualifying cart subtotal ÷ new free-delivery threshold

Cap the displayed progress between 0% and 100%.

Below the progress bar, display:

“Add [FORMATTED DIFFERENCE] more to unlock free delivery.”

Calculate the missing amount as:

new free-delivery threshold − qualifying cart subtotal

Add this small note at the bottom:

“Standard delivery fees will apply if your subtotal remains below [FORMATTED NEW THRESHOLD].”

Use the customer’s selected currency and the website’s existing currency-formatting system.

ACTIONS

Primary button:

“Continue shopping”

Behavior:
Close the popup and keep the customer on the current page.

Secondary text link:

“Continue to cart”

Behavior:
Close the popup and open the cart.

Close icon:

Close the popup without reverting the selected delivery city.

DESIGN

Match the existing Presentail city-selection popup and website theme:

- Dark translucent overlay behind the popup.
- Centered white modal on desktop.
- Approximately 540px maximum width.
- 22px rounded corners.
- Subtle shadow.
- 28–32px internal padding.
- Presentail dark teal #00414e.
- Elegant serif heading matching existing website headings.
- Existing website sans-serif font for body copy.
- Small outlined delivery-truck icon inside a pale beige circle.
- Light-gray bordered threshold summary box.
- Slim dark-teal progress bar.
- Full-width dark-teal primary button with white text.
- Centered underlined secondary action.
- Close icon in the top-right.
- No red warning colors, warning triangles, illustrations or product images.
- Lock background scrolling while open.

MOBILE

Below 768px:

- Display the popup as a bottom sheet attached directly to the bottom of the viewport.
- Use 100% width.
- Round the top corners only.
- Respect mobile safe-area padding.
- Keep the primary button full width.
- Allow internal vertical scrolling on short screens.
- Prevent horizontal scrolling.
- The bottom sheet must reach the physical bottom of the screen without a visible gap.

ACCESSIBILITY

- Use an accessible dialog with aria-modal="true".
- Move focus into the popup when it opens.
- Trap keyboard focus inside it.
- Support closing with Escape.
- Restore focus to the city selector after closing.
- Add accessible labels to the close button and actions.
- Maintain WCAG-compliant color contrast.

STATE AND ERROR HANDLING

- Show the warning once per qualifying city-change event.
- Prevent duplicate popup instances.
- Do not reopen it because of component rerenders.
- Recalculate eligibility whenever the customer changes city again.
- Prefer backend-calculated eligibility so the popup and checkout cannot disagree.
- If threshold retrieval fails, keep the city change but do not display inaccurate information.
- Do not modify unrelated cart, checkout, city-selection or delivery-fee behavior.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- The popup appears only when changing cities causes the cart to lose free-delivery eligibility.
- The city, subtotal, threshold, missing amount and progress percentage are accurate.
- Values update correctly for every currency.
- Closing the popup does not revert the selected city.
- “Continue shopping” stays on the current page.
- “Continue to cart” opens the cart.
- Checkout displays the same eligibility and delivery fee shown by the popup.
- The desktop and mobile layouts match the supplied design.
- Existing delivery and checkout behavior continues working.
