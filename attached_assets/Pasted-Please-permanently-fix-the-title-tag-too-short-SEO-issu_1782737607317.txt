Please permanently fix the “title tag too short” SEO issue.

Audit file:
title_tag_too_short(1).csv

Issue:
The audit found 56 pages with title tags that are too short.

Important pattern:
All 56 issues are contact pages.

Examples of current short titles:

* Contact Presentail in Aley
* Contact Presentail in Metn
* Contact Presentail in Dubai
* Contacter Presentail à Tyr
* تواصل مع Presentail في دبي

Current root cause:
We previously shortened the contact page title template too much to fix the “title tag too long” issue. Now the title is under the SEO audit’s minimum length threshold.

Goal:
Create a balanced contact page title template that is:

* not too short
* not too long
* city-specific
* language-specific
* useful for SEO
* safe for long city names

Do not change URLs, canonicals, sitemap, hreflang, or page routing in this task. Only fix the contact page title template and title-length guardrail.

2. Add smart title length guardrails

Please add logic in the SEO helper to prevent contact titles from becoming too short or too long again.

Recommended title length rules:

* Target length: 35–60 characters
* Too short warning: under 30 characters
* Too long warning: over 60 characters
* Hard maximum: 65 characters

For normal city names, use the preferred templates above.

If the title becomes over 65 characters because the city name is very long, use a shorter fallback:

English fallback:
Contact Presentail in {City}

French fallback:
Contacter Presentail à {City}

Arabic fallback:
تواصل مع Presentail في {City}

If the fallback becomes too short, use the medium template:
English:
Presentail Contact in {City} | Delivery Help

French:
Contact Presentail {City} | Aide livraison

Arabic:
تواصل مع Presentail {City} | دعم التوصيل
