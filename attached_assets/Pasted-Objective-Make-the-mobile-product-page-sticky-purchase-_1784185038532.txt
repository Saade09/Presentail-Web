Objective
Make the mobile product-page sticky purchase bar more informative by keeping the product price visible when the customer has scrolled past the main price.

Approved change
- Change the sticky CTA label from “ADD TO CART” to “ADD TO CART · $360” for the referenced product.
- Implement this dynamically as: “ADD TO CART · {current formatted product price}”.
- This is the sticky bottom purchase bar, not the website footer.

Scope
- Mobile product detail pages.
- Preserve the existing dark-teal rounded button, cart icon, fixed positioning, spacing, height, and overall styling.
- Keep the cart icon to the left and visually center the complete CTA content.
- Do not redesign or change any other product-page component.

Functional requirements
- Display the active selling price in the shopper’s selected currency.
- Use the same currency conversion, rounding, sale-price, and formatting rules as the primary product price.
- Update the sticky price immediately if the selected option or configuration changes the product price.
- Keep the CTA action and Add to Cart behavior unchanged.
- Use a centered dot separator: “·”.
- Continue using the existing localized Add to Cart translation for EN, AR, and FR; the price remains dynamically formatted.
- Ensure the Arabic layout and content order remain natural in RTL.

Responsive requirements
- Keep the label on one line for supported mobile widths.
- Handle short and long price formats without clipping, overflow, or reducing the tap target.
- Respect iPhone safe-area spacing.
- Button must remain at least 44 px tall and fully tappable.

Acceptance criteria
- Product page shows “ADD TO CART · {price}” in the sticky bottom CTA.
- The displayed price always matches the current primary product price.
- Currency changes update both prices consistently.
- Sale or configurable prices display the correct active price.
- CTA remains centered, readable, and on one line across supported mobile devices.
- Existing Add to Cart behavior is unchanged.
- No desktop or unrelated page changes are introduced.

QA cases
- Standard product: $360.
- Low-price product.
- Four-digit AED price.
- Long LBP price format.
- Sale-price product.
- EN, AR, and FR.
- iPhone safe area and smallest supported mobile viewport.
