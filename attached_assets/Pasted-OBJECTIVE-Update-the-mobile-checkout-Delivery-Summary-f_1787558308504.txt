OBJECTIVE
Update the mobile checkout Delivery Summary for customers who have deliberately selected Midnight Delivery. Respect the customer’s selection and remove the Express Delivery upsell from checkout.

FINAL DESIGN
Use the attached image as the approved visual reference. This is the version to implement.

SCOPE
- Mobile checkout experience.
- Delivery Summary section when Midnight Delivery is currently selected.
- Sticky checkout summary at the bottom of the screen.
- Do not change Midnight Delivery eligibility, availability, pricing configuration, checkout totals, or underlying delivery-slot logic beyond what is required to represent the selected service correctly.
- Preserve the “Delivery Summary” title on mobile.
- The Order Summary remains directly below Delivery Summary in the normal checkout flow.

DELIVERY SUMMARY UI
- Keep the existing white rounded Delivery Summary container and the “Delivery Summary” heading.
- Show one selected Midnight Delivery card only.
- Completely remove/hide the Express Delivery suggestion/upgrade card when Midnight Delivery is selected.
- Use the approved subtle warm cream background for the Midnight card. This is intentional: it identifies Midnight Delivery as a special/premium service without making it look like an error or warning.
- Retain the existing Presentail dark-teal color for the crescent-moon icon, price, and action.
- Keep spacing compact and consistent with the approved image; the container should wrap its content naturally with no unnecessary blank space inside it.

APPROVED CONTENT AND HIERARCHY
- Service label: “Midnight delivery”
- Primary delivery promise: “Arrives tonight, 11 PM–1 AM”
- Supporting description: “Special midnight delivery window”
- Price: “+$20” using the actual configured Midnight Delivery fee
- Action: “Change”
- Use the crescent-moon icon shown in the reference.
- The arrival promise must remain dynamic. Use the existing selected date/slot and market timezone logic; do not hardcode “tonight” or the displayed hours when another date/window applies.
- The displayed fee must come from checkout pricing; do not hardcode $20.

CHANGE ACTION
- Tapping “Change” must reopen the existing delivery date/time selection interface.
- Express Delivery can still be available within that delivery-selection interface when eligible.
- Do not promote or re-suggest Express inside checkout after the customer has returned with Midnight Delivery selected.

STICKY MOBILE CHECKOUT BAR
- Replace generic/incorrect “Standard delivery today” wording when Midnight Delivery is selected.
- Approved summary pattern: “1 item · Midnight delivery tonight”
- Item count must remain dynamic and use correct singular/plural behavior.
- “Tonight”/date wording must be derived from the selected delivery date.
- Preserve the Total display and checkout CTA.
- Ensure the displayed total includes the configured Midnight Delivery fee and remains synchronized with Order Summary.

ORDER SUMMARY
- Keep Order Summary directly below Delivery Summary in the normal page sequence.
- Continue showing the base/standard delivery component and Midnight Delivery surcharge according to existing pricing logic.
- Example from the approved state: Items $307 + Standard delivery Free + Midnight Delivery $20 = Total $327.
- Do not duplicate or omit the Midnight fee.
- Do not create artificial blank vertical space between Delivery Summary and Order Summary.

RESPONSIVE / VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
- Match the approved reference on common iPhone/mobile viewport widths.
- Prevent wrapping/collision between the delivery promise, price, and “Change” action.
- Preserve accessible tap targets, contrast, and readable type sizes.
- Keep long translated text and different currency formats in mind.
- Avoid fixed heights that create empty space or clip localized content.

STATES TO VERIFY
- Midnight Delivery selected for tonight.
- Midnight Delivery selected for a future date, if supported.
- Different configured Midnight fees/currencies.
- One item and multiple items.
- Express eligible and ineligible.
- Free and paid standard/base delivery.
- User taps Change and selects a different delivery option.
- Refresh/back navigation preserves the selected option and correct total.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
1. When Midnight Delivery is selected, no Express upsell appears in Delivery Summary.
2. The “Delivery Summary” title remains visible on mobile.
3. The selected Midnight card matches the attached approved design, including cream styling and crescent icon.
4. Service name, dynamic arrival window, supporting copy, fee, and Change action are correct.
5. Sticky bar identifies Midnight Delivery rather than Standard Delivery.
6. Order Summary follows directly below and calculates the total correctly with the Midnight surcharge once.
7. “Change” opens the delivery selector and existing eligible delivery choices remain available there.
8. Layout is compact, responsive, accessible, and has no unnecessary inner or inter-section whitespace.
9. Existing non-Midnight checkout behavior is not regressed.
10. QA is completed on mobile Safari and Chrome at representative mobile widths.
