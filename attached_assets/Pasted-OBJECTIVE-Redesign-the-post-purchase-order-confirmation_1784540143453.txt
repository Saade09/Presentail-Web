OBJECTIVE

Redesign the post-purchase order confirmation page for desktop and mobile while preserving the existing route, dynamic order data, payment logic, analytics, translations, and actions.

DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

- Use a completely white background.
- Remove decorative artwork, patterns, floral graphics, colored backgrounds, header, and footer.
- Remove the order tracking/progress steps entirely.
- Use one centered vertical column on desktop, approximately 760px wide.
- Use one stacked column on mobile with 16px side padding and no horizontal overflow.
- Keep Presentail teal #00404D, Playfair Display headings, Inter body text, subtle borders, restrained rounding, and little or no shadow.

SECTION ORDER

1. Confirmation area
2. Your gift
3. Card message
4. Delivery date and time
5. Recipient details
6. Delivery address
7. Order summary
8. Actions

CONFIRMATION AREA

- Show a compact teal checkmark, “Order confirmed,” the reassurance message, and the dynamic order reference.
- Keep the section compact with no decorative visuals.

YOUR GIFT

- Show every product image, name, quantity, and line total in the paid currency.
- Support multiple products and long product names.

CARD MESSAGE

- Show recipient, message, and sender in a simple read-only bordered area.
- Hide the section when no message exists.

DELIVERY AND RECIPIENT INFORMATION

- Show the formatted delivery date and time slot.
- Add “Recipient details” with the receiver’s dynamic name and phone number.
- Add “Delivery address” with the complete formatted address, including building, street, area, city, country, floor, apartment, and delivery instructions when available.
- Omit unavailable optional fields instead of showing blanks.
- Do not expose coordinates, raw timestamps, database IDs, or internal location codes.

ORDER SUMMARY

- Place it underneath the preceding sections in the same column, never beside them or sticky.
- Show all applicable financial rows, final total, and payment method.
- Use original formatted amounts only.
- Do not recalculate totals or show an approximate USD conversion.

ACTIONS

- Full-width “Continue shopping” button.
- “View my orders” secondary link.
- “Need help? Contact us” support action.
- Reuse existing routes and actions.

IMPLEMENTATION SAFEGUARDS

- Preserve checkout, payment, order creation, success analytics, localization, and RTL support.
- Ensure success analytics do not fire again during rerenders.
- Reuse existing components, formatters, icons, and design tokens.
- Do not add mock production data or unnecessary dependencies.

VERIFICATION

- Test desktop and mobile layouts.
- Test one and multiple products, long names, international phone numbers, long addresses, missing optional fields, no card message, discounts, wallet deductions, free and paid delivery, multiple payment methods, English, and Arabic RTL.
- Confirm there is no horizontal overflow, console error, or TypeScript error.
