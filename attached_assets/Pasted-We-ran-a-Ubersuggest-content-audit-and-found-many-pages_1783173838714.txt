We ran a Ubersuggest content audit and found many pages with low word count.

Audit file:
ubersuggest site audit presentail.com content_count_words.csv

Issue:
The audit shows 889 URLs with low word count. These are mostly not product/category pages. They are mainly city/static template pages repeated across languages and cities.

Main affected page types:

* Homepage
* City pages
* Brands pages
* Contact pages
* Corporate pages
* FAQs pages
* Occasions pages
* Shop pages
* Weddings pages

Goal:
Improve low-content SEO pages by adding useful, customer-friendly visible content sections. Do not add spammy text. Do not simply hide text for SEO. Content should be visible, helpful, and aligned with Presentail’s luxury ecommerce style.

Important:
This task is about improving on-page content quality and word count. Do not change URLs, slugs, canonicals, hreflang, sitemap, or metadata unless absolutely required.

1. Investigate the audit file

Please review:
ubersuggest site audit presentail.com content_count_words.csv

Group the affected URLs by:

* language/country prefix
* city
* page type
* current word count

Confirm the main patterns:

* city homepages are thin
* brands pages are thin
* contact pages are thin
* corporate pages are thin
* FAQs pages are thin
* occasions listing pages are thin
* shop pages are thin
* weddings pages are thin

2. Create a reusable ContentQualitySection system

Please create reusable on-page content sections by page type.

These sections should be:

* visible to users
* server-rendered or present in initial HTML where possible
* elegant and not spammy
* responsive on mobile
* multilingual-ready
* dynamic by city, country, language, and page type

Do not create one giant generic paragraph reused everywhere. Each page type should have its own content structure.

3. Homepage content improvement

The homepage currently has very low word count.

Add a clean homepage SEO/content section lower on the page, not above the main shopping content.

Suggested sections:

* “Send Flowers & Gifts Online with Presentail”
* Short paragraph about flowers, cakes, balloons, plants, chocolates and gifts
* “Available in Lebanon, UAE and Cyprus”
* Short benefits row:

  * Same-day delivery options
  * Flowers, cakes & gifts
  * Recipient address collection
  * Online ordering and tracking
* Optional short FAQ section

Homepage content should be around 250–400 useful words total across the page, not one huge block.

12. Multilingual support

All content templates should support:

* English
* French
* Arabic

Rules:

* English pages should show English content.
* French pages should show French content.
* Arabic pages should show Arabic content and RTL layout.
* Do not show English SEO paragraphs on Arabic/French pages if translation support exists.
* Use existing i18n system if available.

13. Avoid duplicate-content risk

Because many pages are city/language versions of the same template, make the content dynamic using:

* city name
* country
* available categories
* available occasions
* available brands
* delivery options
* city-specific internal links

Do not create identical paragraphs across every city page except for one city name if avoidable.

Where possible, include dynamic content based on actual availability:

* categories available in the city
* brands available in the city
* occasions available in the city
* delivery options available in the city

14. Design requirements

Use the same visual direction already used for category and occasion SEO sections:

* full-width light background band
* centered inner content container
* elegant H2
* short readable paragraphs
* lightweight benefit cards
* internal link chips
* FAQ accordion
* mobile-friendly layout

Do not add long text blocks above products or above the main conversion area.

18. Acceptance criteria

This task is complete when:

* Low-word-count pages have useful visible content sections.
* Homepage has stronger visible content.
* City pages have city-specific content.
* Shop pages have shopping-specific content.
* Brands pages have brand/gifting-specific content.
* Corporate pages have corporate gifting content.
* Weddings pages have wedding flower content.
* FAQs pages have useful visible FAQs.
* Occasions listing pages have occasion-focused content.
* Content appears in the initial HTML where possible.
* Content is not hidden purely for SEO.
* Content does not hurt shopping/conversion UX.
* Mobile layout is clean.
* FAQ schema only appears when FAQs are visible.
* No routing, canonical, sitemap, or hreflang behavior is broken.
* The system is reusable and can later connect to Presentail OS/admin content.
