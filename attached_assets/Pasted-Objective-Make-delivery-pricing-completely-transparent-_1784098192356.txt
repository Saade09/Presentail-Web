Objective

Make delivery pricing completely transparent inside the delivery-option cards on mobile product pages. Customers must understand the standard delivery fee, express add-on, total express cost and whether their current order qualifies for free standard delivery before adding the product to the cart.

The displayed amounts must be calculated dynamically from the customer’s current cart, selected product and quantity, delivery area, currency and delivery configuration. Do not hard-code the Beirut example values.

Scope

- Apply the approved design to mobile product pages using the existing mobile breakpoint.
- Implement it in the shared product-page delivery component so it works across every product, country, city and delivery area.
- Preserve the existing desktop/tablet experience unless the same component already shares pricing logic. Logic should be reusable across breakpoints, but this task’s approved visual treatment is mobile.
- Keep the existing delivery option structure, icons, radio states and selected-card outline.
- Preserve the approved reassurance tiles, compact payment/Trustpilot card and sticky Add to Cart bar.

Required pricing inputs

Use the existing backend/configuration sources for:

- Current cart subtotal
- Current product price
- Selected variation, add-ons and quantity
- Applicable discounts and promotions
- Selected country
- Selected city/delivery area
- Standard delivery fee
- Free standard delivery threshold
- Express delivery availability
- Express add-on fee
- Currency and currency formatting
- Tax treatment

The product-page calculation should show the expected delivery state after adding the currently selected product and quantity to the existing cart.

Do not duplicate delivery-pricing rules in the frontend. Use the same pricing service or calculation logic used by cart and checkout so all surfaces remain consistent.

State 1: Order does not qualify for free standard delivery

Example configuration:

- Product/cart value: $85
- Standard delivery fee: $9
- Express add-on: $15
- Express total delivery fee: $24
- Free standard delivery threshold: $90

Selected Express Delivery card:

- Keep title: “Express Delivery”
- Keep helper: “Arrives in 90 minutes” or the configured express ETA.
- Show the total delivery charge on the right: “$24 total”
- Show a smaller breakdown directly below: “$9 delivery + $15 express”
- Keep the checked radio and selected outline.

Schedule delivery card:

- Use title: “Schedule delivery”
- Show the selected date/time when one exists.
- Before selection, show: “Choose a date and time”.
- Show the standard delivery fee on the right: “$9”
- Show smaller supporting text: “Standard delivery”
- Keep the unselected or selected radio state as applicable.

Helper copy beneath the cards:

“Fees update based on your cart total and the recipient’s area.”

Use a small muted information icon and subdued neutral text.

State 2: Order qualifies for free standard delivery

When the calculated order value meets the configured free-delivery threshold, update both cards immediately.

Selected Express Delivery card:

- Show only the express add-on as the total: “$15 total”
- Show the smaller breakdown: “Free standard + $15 express”
- Do not include the waived standard fee in the displayed total.

Schedule delivery card:

- Replace the standard fee with “FREE”.
- Display FREE using Presentail teal or an approved success green.
- Keep the smaller supporting text: “Standard delivery”.

Replace the neutral helper message with a positive inline confirmation:

“Your order qualifies for free standard delivery.”

- Add a small teal/green checkmark.
- Keep the treatment compact and inline.
- Do not add a large banner or progress bar.

State transitions

Pricing must update without a full-page refresh when any relevant input changes, including:

- Product quantity
- Selected product variation
- Product add-ons
- Existing cart contents
- Promotion or discount application
- Currency
- Country
- City or delivery area
- Delivery option
- Free-delivery configuration

If the cart drops below the threshold, immediately return from the FREE state to the regular standard-fee state.

If the cart crosses the threshold, immediately waive only the configured standard delivery fee. The express add-on remains payable unless the delivery configuration explicitly waives it.

Variable delivery fees

- Delivery fees may vary by country, city, district or delivery area.
- Display exact pricing whenever the recipient’s delivery area is known.
- If the exact delivery area has not yet been selected, use the existing delivery configuration to show “From {{amount}}” only when a reliable minimum exists.
- Otherwise show: “Calculated after selecting the recipient’s area.”
- Never display an exact fee that may be incorrect for the customer’s eventual area.
- Recalculate immediately once the area becomes known.

Configuration requirements

Do not hard-code:

- $9 standard delivery
- $15 express add-on
- $90 free-delivery threshold
- 90-minute ETA
- USD currency
- Beirut

All values must come from the relevant city/delivery-area and currency configuration.

Formatting

- Use the customer’s selected currency.
- Follow Presentail’s existing currency-symbol and rounding rules.
- Do not display unnecessary decimal places.
- Clearly distinguish the product price from delivery fees.
- Ensure totals shown on the product page match cart and checkout exactly.
- Use localized number and currency formatting for English, Arabic and French.

Delivery-option behavior

- Only one delivery option may be selected at a time.
- Selecting Express must update the expected delivery total and sticky/cart state.
- Selecting Schedule delivery must open or reveal the existing date/time selection flow.
- Only show the date/time line after a valid slot has been selected.
- If Express is unavailable for the selected area or time, hide or disable it using the existing availability rules and accessible explanatory copy.
- If no valid delivery options exist, prevent Add to Cart and show the appropriate availability message.

Sticky Add to Cart and cart integration

- Do not redesign the approved sticky Add to Cart bar in this task.
- When the product is added, persist the selected delivery method and selected scheduled slot where applicable.
- Revalidate pricing when adding to cart to prevent stale totals.
- If the fee changes during validation, update the displayed price and explain the change before completing the action.

Accessibility

- Delivery prices and fee breakdowns must be readable by screen readers.
- Do not communicate the free state through color alone; include the word FREE and the qualification message.
- Maintain appropriate text contrast.
- Keep radio controls and delivery cards keyboard accessible.
- Ensure pricing text does not interfere with the radio control’s touch target.
- Maintain at least a 44px touch target for selectable delivery cards.

Responsive requirements

- Prevent price labels and breakdowns from overlapping titles, helper text or radio controls.
- Support mobile widths down to at least 320px.
- Permit sensible wrapping of the breakdown text on narrow screens without increasing card height excessively.
- Do not introduce horizontal page scrolling.
- Preserve spacing between delivery cards, helper message, reassurance tiles and payment section.

Analytics

Track delivery-option interaction through the existing analytics setup:

- Delivery pricing viewed
- Express delivery selected
- Scheduled delivery selected
- Free standard delivery qualified
- Free standard delivery qualification lost
- Delivery price recalculated

Where available, include:

- Product ID
- Country/city/delivery-area ID
- Currency
- Standard delivery fee
- Express add-on
- Free-delivery threshold
- Whether free standard delivery applied
- Selected delivery method

Error and fallback handling

- If pricing cannot be calculated, do not display $0 or FREE.
- Show: “Delivery fee calculated at checkout” as a temporary safe fallback.
- Log the pricing failure with sufficient context for debugging.
- Reattempt calculation when relevant inputs change.
- Cart and checkout remain the authoritative final validation points.

Acceptance criteria

- Mobile product pages display the standard delivery fee and express pricing directly inside the delivery cards.
- Express pricing clearly shows both the total and its fee breakdown.
- Scheduled delivery clearly shows the standard delivery price.
- When the order qualifies, scheduled delivery changes to FREE and Express displays only the express add-on as the total.
- The qualification confirmation appears only while the order meets the configured threshold.
- Pricing updates without a full refresh when cart value, quantity, variation, discount, currency or delivery area changes.
- No country, area, fee, threshold, ETA or currency is hard-coded.
- Product-page delivery pricing exactly matches cart and checkout.
- Variable-fee areas never receive a misleading exact price.
- English, Arabic and French layouts work without overlap or truncation.
- The experience works at mobile widths down to 320px.
- Existing reassurance tiles, compact payment/Trustpilot section and sticky Add to Cart design remain unchanged.
- Desktop and tablet layouts do not receive unintended visual changes.
