OBJECTIVE
Increase product browsing density on mobile collection/PLP pages by tightening the vertical spacing between product rows, while preserving Presentail's clean, premium visual feel.

TARGET SPACING
- Product image to product name: approximately 12-16px.
- Product name to price: approximately 4-6px.
- Price / bottom of product information to next product-row image: approximately 24-32px.
- These are starting targets, not rigid values. Fine-tune by about +/-4px during visual QA if needed, but the result should be materially denser than current production.

IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
1. Reduce excessive whitespace after each product's price before the next row begins.
2. Keep product image, name and price visually grouped as one card.
3. Control spacing at the grid/row level where possible instead of stacking arbitrary margins on individual card elements.
4. Ensure the two products in each row align cleanly when one product name wraps to two lines and the other stays on one line.
5. Different title lengths must not create staggered/inconsistent starting positions for the following row.
6. Do not shrink product photography or materially change horizontal column spacing.
7. Do not change typography, badges, prices, card functionality or the two-column grid unless required to correct spacing behavior.

DESIGN INTENT
Follow the approved tighter 'After' mockup: more products visible per scroll, but the PLP should still feel premium rather than compressed or marketplace-like. Preserve comfortable separation between distinct product rows.

RESPONSIVE REQUIREMENTS
- Prioritize mobile widths around 375-430px.
- Verify one-line, two-line and unusually long product names.
- Verify sale-price, badge and other existing card states do not break row alignment.
- Desktop/tablet must not regress; use mobile-specific spacing rules where appropriate.
- No horizontal overflow or layout shift.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
- Vertical distance between consecutive product rows is visibly reduced versus production.
- Price-to-next-image spacing is approximately 24-32px on standard mobile cards.
- Image-to-name spacing is approximately 12-16px.
- Name-to-price spacing is approximately 4-6px.
- Products remain easy to distinguish from the next row.
- Both columns maintain consistent row alignment even with different product-name lengths.
- More product content is visible within the same viewport/scroll distance.
- No changes to product image dimensions, card click behavior, pricing logic, badges or product data.
- Result retains the existing premium Presentail aesthetic.

QA
- Compare production vs staging at identical viewport dimensions.
- Test iPhone/Safari and Android/Chrome-sized viewports.
- Test one-line and two-line names in the same row.
- Test Best Seller/badge cards and cards without badges.
- Test discounted/sale products if applicable.
- Scroll through multiple rows to ensure spacing stays consistent.
- Check several collection pages, not only Flower Boxes.

DELIVERABLE
Implement on staging and attach before/after screenshots at the same mobile viewport size. Include at least 3 consecutive product rows so the density improvement and row consistency can be reviewed before production deployment.
