OBJECTIVE

Improve checkout behavior when a customer changes the delivery district after already selecting a delivery option or time slot. The customer must clearly understand when the original delivery selection is no longer valid, what changed, and what action is required before payment.

CURRENT PROBLEM

- A customer can select a delivery option for one district, such as Express delivery in Beirut.
- During checkout, the customer can change Delivery District, for example from Beirut to Akkar.
- The selected slot can become unavailable and the delivery fee and total can change.
- The current checkout clears the delivery details but does not clearly explain why.
- The payment CTA can still look active even though a new delivery selection is required.

REQUIRED BEHAVIOR

1. Whenever the customer changes the delivery district, immediately revalidate the currently selected delivery method, date, and time slot against the new district.
2. Recalculate the delivery fee and order total using the new district before presenting the next state.
3. Never silently replace the customer's selected delivery option with another option or time slot.
4. If the existing delivery selection remains valid, preserve it. Do not force unnecessary reselection.
5. If the selection remains valid but its delivery fee changes, preserve the selection, update the fee and total, and clearly notify the customer that the delivery price was updated.
6. If the previous selection is not available in the new district, clear the invalid delivery method/date/time state and require the customer to choose again.
7. Do not allow the customer to proceed to payment while delivery details are invalid or missing.

APPROVED INVALID-SELECTION UI — LEFT COLUMN

- Place a compact persistent inline notification directly below the Delivery District dropdown and above the Delivery Details address field.
- Use a pale amber background, subtle amber border, rounded corners, a small warning/info icon, and readable dark text consistent with the existing Presentail checkout design system.
- The notice must remain visible until a valid delivery option is selected. Do not use a temporary toast as the only notification.
- Title: “Delivery options updated for {{District}}”
- Body for the Beirut → Akkar example: “Express delivery isn’t available in this district. Please choose a new delivery time.”
- Use the selected district dynamically; do not hard-code Akkar.
- Shift the address field downward naturally without overlapping or breaking the Recipient Details card.

APPROVED INVALID-SELECTION UI — ORDER SUMMARY

- Replace the normal neutral Delivery summary card with a pale amber required-action state.
- Keep the small uppercase DELIVERY label.
- Heading: “Delivery selection required”
- Supporting text: “Choose an available time for {{District}}.”
- Provide a clear outlined action: “Choose delivery time”
- Clicking the action must open the existing delivery date/time selector with only options valid for the newly selected district.
- Keep the recalculated delivery fee and total visible above this card.
- Disable and visually mute the “Continue to Payment” button until a valid delivery method/date/time is selected.
- The disabled CTA must not navigate, submit, or briefly allow a request through while delivery options are loading.

WHEN ONLY THE DELIVERY FEE CHANGES

- Keep the existing valid delivery selection.
- Update the delivery fee and total immediately.
- Show a compact informational notice explaining that the delivery fee was updated for the new district, including the previous and new fee where available.
- Do not disable payment if all required delivery details remain valid.

LOADING AND ERROR HANDLING

- While revalidating delivery availability, show a short loading state in the Delivery summary and temporarily disable the payment CTA to prevent submission with stale data.
- If the availability request fails, keep payment disabled and show a recoverable inline error with a Retry action.
- Do not display the old delivery promise, old arrival time, or old fee after the district has changed.
- Protect against rapid district changes and out-of-order API responses; only the latest selected district may update checkout state.

SCOPE

- Implement on desktop and mobile checkout.
- Apply consistently across all markets where changing delivery geography can affect availability, timing, or price.
- Use the market’s existing terminology, such as Delivery District or Delivery Emirate, while keeping the underlying behavior shared.
- Preserve the current checkout visual system and do not redesign unrelated Recipient Details or Order Summary elements.

ACCESSIBILITY

- Do not communicate the state using color alone; retain the icon, heading, and explanatory text.
- Announce the delivery-selection change to screen readers using an appropriate live region.
- Move focus to the persistent notification or required-action delivery card after an invalid selection is cleared, without trapping focus.
- The disabled CTA and Choose delivery time action must have correct keyboard and focus behavior.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- Beirut Express → Akkar: Express is cleared, the new delivery fee and total appear, both persistent warning states appear, and payment remains disabled until the customer selects an Akkar-valid delivery time.
- Changing to a district where the same slot is valid: the slot remains selected.
- Changing to a district where the slot is valid but the fee differs: the slot remains selected, the fee and total update, and the customer is notified.
- Selecting a new valid slot removes the warning state, updates the Delivery summary, and re-enables payment when all other required fields are valid.
- Returning to the original district does not automatically restore an old invalidated slot; the customer must select a valid option again unless the current selection remained valid throughout.
- Refreshing or navigating back must not restore a delivery selection that is invalid for the currently selected district.
- No stale district, delivery promise, fee, or total is submitted to the order/payment API.
- Verify desktop and mobile in English, Arabic, and French, including long district names and right-to-left layout.

QA SCENARIOS

- Beirut → Akkar with Express selected.
- District A → District B with the same standard slot still available.
- District change that only changes the delivery fee.
- District change while the availability request is still loading.
- Rapidly switching between three districts.
- Availability API failure and successful retry.
- Mobile sticky CTA and desktop Order Summary CTA.
- Authenticated checkout and guest checkout.
