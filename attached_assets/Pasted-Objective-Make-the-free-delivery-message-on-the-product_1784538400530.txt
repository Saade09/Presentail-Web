Objective
Make the free-delivery message on the product page clearer and remove the impression that free standard delivery and the Express delivery fee contradict each other.

Required changes
1. Replace the current copy “Free standard delivery applied to your order.”
2. When the order qualifies for free standard delivery and standard delivery is selected, display:
   “You qualify for free standard delivery.”
3. When the order qualifies for free standard delivery but Express Delivery is selected, display:
   “Free standard delivery included — you’re only paying the $15 express upgrade.”
4. Keep the existing check icon, but ensure the message remains clearly visible below the product price on both mobile and desktop.
5. The copy must update dynamically when the customer switches between Express Delivery and scheduled standard delivery.
6. Do not show the free-delivery qualification message when the basket does not meet the applicable city’s free-delivery threshold; preserve the existing paid-delivery/progress logic in that case.
7. Support the final copy in English, Arabic, and French using the website’s existing translation system. Do not hard-code the $15 amount; use the actual localized Express upgrade fee configured for the selected city and currency.

Acceptance criteria
- A qualifying order with standard delivery selected shows: “You qualify for free standard delivery.”
- A qualifying order with Express selected clearly states that standard delivery is free and only the Express upgrade is being charged.
- The Express fee in the message always matches the fee shown in the selected delivery option and cart total.
- Switching delivery options immediately updates the message without refreshing the page.
- Non-qualifying orders never see the qualification message.
- Copy and layout are verified on mobile and desktop in English, Arabic, and French.
- No changes are made to delivery eligibility, fee calculations, or cart pricing; this task changes presentation and dynamic messaging only.
