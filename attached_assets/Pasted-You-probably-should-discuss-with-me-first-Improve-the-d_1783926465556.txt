You probably should discuss with me first

Improve the delivery selector on the product page so customers can understand and select an available delivery window more quickly, while preserving the current Presentail design.
Current issue
The delivery selector works, but the available dates and time ranges require extra interpretation. The More button is also unclear, and customers do not receive a simple confirmation of their final selection.
Required improvements
1. Clarify the date options
Keep the existing pill-based layout, but display each option consistently:
Tomorrow with 13 Jul underneath
Tue with 14 Jul underneath
Replace More with Other date and retain the calendar icon
Selecting Other date should open the existing date picker.
2. Simplify time formatting
Display each delivery window as a concise range:
9 AM–2 PM
2 PM–6 PM
6 PM–10 PM
9 PM–11 PM
Avoid displaying the start and end times as two disconnected times.
3. Strengthen the selected state
The selected date and time should use:
Presentail’s dark-teal background
White text
A subtle checkmark
Clear contrast from unselected options
Only one date and one delivery window may be selected at a time.
4. Add a selection confirmation
Show a compact confirmation line at the bottom of the card after both selections are made:
Delivery: Tomorrow, 13 Jul · 9 AM–2 PM
This should update immediately when the customer changes the date or time.
5. Show delivery fees clearly
If a delivery window has an additional fee, display it directly within or below the option:
9 AM–2 PM · Free
6 PM–10 PM · +€15
Fees must update dynamically based on:
Selected city or district
Order value
Free-delivery eligibility
Standard versus express delivery
Selected currency
Special-day delivery rules
Do not hardcode prices or thresholds.
Visual requirements
Preserve the current card, typography, spacing and pill styling.
Do not introduce a completely new delivery-selector design.
Keep the selector compact and mobile-friendly.
Maintain sufficiently large touch targets.
Avoid increasing the overall height unnecessarily.
Ensure longer translated text does not overflow.
Behaviour requirements
Default to the earliest available delivery date and time.
Never show expired or unavailable windows.
Update availability when the customer changes their city or district.
Clearly disable unavailable options rather than allowing selection.
Revalidate the selected window before adding the product to the cart.
If availability changes, explain the issue and prompt the customer to select another window.
Preserve the selection when the customer navigates between the product page and cart.
Acceptance criteria
More is replaced with Other date.
Time ranges use a consistent, concise format.
Selected options are immediately recognizable.
A delivery confirmation appears after selection.
Applicable fees are visible before adding the product to the cart.
Delivery dates, availability and fees are dynamically loaded.
The component works in English, Arabic and French.
No unrelated product-page sections are redesigned.
