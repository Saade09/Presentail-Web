Please refine the SEO content section on category and occasion pages.

The module was implemented, but the current design feels too narrow and disconnected from the page. Please update it so the SEO content section uses a full-width background band while keeping the actual content centered and readable.

Important:
Keep the section below the product grid and above the footer. Do not move it above the products.

Current issue:
The SEO content appears as a centered card with a narrow width. On category and occasion pages, this creates too much empty white space on the sides and makes the section feel detached from the page.

Goal:
Make the SEO content section feel like a natural full-width section of the page, similar to a premium ecommerce content block.

New design direction:

1. Full-width section background

The SEO content section should span the full viewport width.

Use a very subtle background color, for example:

* warm off-white
* very light beige
* very light grey
* #FAF8F5 or similar

It should visually separate the SEO section from the product grid and footer without feeling heavy.

2. Centered inner container

Inside the full-width background, keep a centered content container.

Recommended:

* max-width: same as the main product page container
* around 1100px–1200px
* horizontal padding on desktop and mobile
* no excessive narrow card feeling

The content should align visually with the product grid area.

3. Remove or soften the boxed card feeling

Avoid the current large floating card look.

Instead:

* use the full-width band as the main background
* optionally keep very subtle cards only for benefit items and FAQ rows
* do not wrap the entire SEO content in a heavy bordered card

The section should feel integrated, not like a popup or separate module.

4. Suggested desktop layout

Use this structure:

Full-width SEO band:

* H2
* intro paragraph
* benefit cards in a 4-column row
* related links as small chips
* FAQ accordion

Example:

5. Spacing

Please adjust vertical spacing so it feels intentional.

Recommended:

* Add enough top margin after the final product row, around 64px–96px desktop
* SEO band padding top/bottom around 64px desktop
* Mobile padding around 32px–40px
* Footer should start immediately after the SEO section with clean spacing

Avoid huge blank gaps between the last product and the SEO section.

6. Benefit cards

Keep benefit cards, but make them lighter.

Desktop:

* 4 columns

Tablet:

* 2 columns

Mobile:

* 1 column or 2 columns if it still looks good

Style:

* subtle border
* small icon
* short title
* short description
* white or transparent card depending on background

Do not make them too tall.

7. Related link chips

Keep the related link chips.

For category pages:
Title:
Popular occasions in {City}

For occasion pages:
Title:
Popular gifts for {Occasion Name}

Style:

* small rounded chips
* subtle border
* hover state
* should wrap nicely on mobile

8. FAQ section

Keep FAQ accordion inside the full-width section.

Style:

* FAQ rows can be white cards or simple bordered rows
* Keep them clean and lightweight
* Full width within the inner content container
* Do not make the FAQ area too visually heavy

FAQ schema should remain only if FAQs are visible.

9. Mobile behavior

On mobile:

* SEO section should be full-width
* Inner content should have comfortable left/right padding
* H2 should not be too large
* Paragraph should be readable
* Benefit cards should stack cleanly
* Related chips should wrap
* FAQ rows should be easy to tap
* No horizontal scrolling

10. Keep existing dynamic logic

Do not remove the existing dynamic functionality.

Keep:

* category-specific H2
* occasion-specific H2
* city-specific text
* dynamic benefits
* dynamic related links
* dynamic FAQs
* FAQ schema when FAQs are visible

11. Do not change SEO logic in this task

This task is visual/layout refinement only.

Do not change:

* page titles
* meta descriptions
* canonicals
* sitemap
* hreflang
* route slugs
* product grid
* filters
* footer links
