OBJECTIVE

Replace the current inline Express-delivery upsell in the Order Summary with a clear delivery-option experience inside Delivery Summary, while preserving the exact “Arrives by [time]” promise.

BACKGROUND / CURRENT ISSUE

The current pre-selection UI shows an underlined sentence in Order Summary (“Need it sooner? Express delivery within 90 min for 55”). It feels disconnected from the selected delivery method and makes the price difficult to interpret.

The selected-Express state can also show Standard and Express as separate charges, which risks duplicate or inconsistent delivery math.

REQUIRED UX

1. STANDARD SELECTED — PAID DELIVERY

Delivery Summary:
- Keep Standard delivery as the selected option.
- Show:
  - “Standard delivery”
  - “Arrives today, 6 PM–10 PM” (dynamic window)
  - “Scheduled delivery window”
  - “Change”
- Directly underneath, show an unselected Express upgrade card:
  - Lightning icon
  - “Express delivery”
  - “Arrives by 7:52 PM” (dynamic exact ETA; never hardcoded)
  - “Within 90 minutes”
  - Incremental price, e.g. “+ 30”
  - “Upgrade” button
- The Express card should use a subtle warm-cream background and muted-teal border, consistent with the current Presentail design system.

Order Summary:
- Show only the currently selected Standard delivery charge.
- Do not add an Express line until the customer selects it.
- Sample state:
  - Items (2): 260
  - Standard delivery: 25
  - Total: 285

2. STANDARD SELECTED — FREE STANDARD DELIVERY

Delivery Summary:
- Mark Standard as FREE and selected.
- Keep the scheduled delivery window visible.
- Show the Express upgrade beneath it with the same exact dynamic ETA.

Pricing rule:
- Calculate the displayed Express upgrade from the customer’s effective delivery price/credit; do not hardcode.
- Formula: Express total fee minus the delivery fee or credit currently applied.
- If the promotion specifically waives Standard only, Express should show +55.
- If the customer has a transferable 25 delivery credit, Express should show +30.

Order Summary:
- Standard delivery: FREE
- Supporting copy: “Free delivery applied”
- Total equals the items subtotal until Express is selected.

3. EXPRESS SELECTED

Delivery Summary:
- Collapse back to one compact selected card.
- Show:
  - “Express delivery”
  - “Arrives by 7:52 PM” (dynamic exact ETA)
  - “Within 90 minutes”
  - “Change”
- Do not show a separate “Selected” label; the card styling is sufficient.

Order Summary:
- Remove the Standard delivery row.
- Show one delivery row only:
  - “Express delivery”: 55
  - Supporting copy: “Arrives by 7:52 PM”
- Recalculate the total immediately.
- Sample state:
  - Items (2): 260
  - Express delivery: 55
  - Total: 315

INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

- Clicking “Upgrade” selects Express, updates Delivery Summary, replaces the delivery row in Order Summary, and updates Total/checkout CTA atomically.
- Clicking “Change” opens the delivery selector and allows switching back to Standard.
- Recalculate the ETA and prices whenever delivery address, delivery date, cart eligibility, promotion, or fulfillment availability changes.
- If an exact ETA is temporarily unavailable, fall back gracefully to “Within 90 minutes”; do not show a stale arrival time.
- Prevent double-clicks/race conditions while the delivery selection is being updated.

COPY

Use these strings unless localization requires an equivalent:
- Standard delivery
- Express delivery
- Arrives today, [window]
- Arrives by [exact time]
- Within 90 minutes
- Scheduled delivery window
- Upgrade
- Change
- FREE
- Free delivery applied

DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

- Preserve Presentail’s existing serif headings, dark teal, white, pale teal, soft grey, rounded corners, and restrained shadows.
- Keep exact arrival time as the strongest line in the Express option.
- Keep Order Summary limited to confirmed charges only.
- Avoid underlined promotional sentences, loud badges, banners, gradients, or duplicate information.
- Ensure the component works cleanly on desktop and mobile.
- All controls must be keyboard-accessible and have clear focus, hover, loading, selected, and disabled states.

ANALYTICS

Track at minimum:
- express_upgrade_impression
- express_upgrade_clicked
- delivery_method_changed
- express_upgrade_success
- express_upgrade_failed

Include cart value, effective Standard fee, Express fee, displayed delta, free-delivery eligibility, ETA, and selected delivery method where permitted.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- The inline Express upsell is removed from Order Summary.
- The Express upgrade is visible in Delivery Summary before selection.
- The Express card shows the exact dynamic “Arrives by” time before and after selection.
- The displayed upgrade amount exactly equals the resulting change in Total.
- Order Summary shows only the selected delivery method.
- Paid Standard, free Standard, and selected Express scenarios all produce correct totals.
- Free-delivery behavior follows the actual promotion/credit rules.
- Exact ETA never becomes stale after address, availability, or cart changes.
- No layout overflow or awkward wrapping at supported desktop/mobile breakpoints.
- Automated tests cover pricing, eligibility, switching, loading/error states, and total reconciliation.
- QA verifies sample totals: 260 + 25 = 285 and 260 + 55 = 315.
