# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public web storefront pages under locale-prefixed routes (`/{lang}-{country}/{city}/...`)
- Landing and marketing pages (`/`, `/careers`, `/blog`, `/partner`, `/weddings`, `/corporate`, `/contact`, `/faqs`, `/terms`, `/privacy`)
- Public catalog and discovery pages (`/shop`, `/product/:slug`, `/brand/:slug`, `/brands`, `/category/:slug`, `/occasion/:slug`, `/occasions`)
- Public shared wishlist pages (`/favorites/share/:token`)
- Crawlability files and SEO infrastructure (`robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, favicon assets, manifest, SEO injection)

## Out of scope
- Authenticated account pages (`/account/**`)
- Checkout funnel pages (`/cart`, `/checkout`, `/order-confirmed`)
- Sign-in and sign-up flows unless they create SEO side effects
- Mobile Expo app surfaces

## Target audience
- Shoppers looking for luxury flower and gift delivery in Lebanon, the UAE, and Cyprus.

## Primary keywords
- Luxury flower delivery
- Gift delivery
- Flowers and gifts in Lebanon
- Flowers and gifts in Dubai / UAE
- Flowers and gifts in Cyprus
